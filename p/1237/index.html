پرامپت فارسی

A very dark, black-and-white iPhone selfie—a vertical shot taken without flash. The image is slightly grainy and has a touch of motion blur, as if captured while moving; it is a spontaneous, candid shot rather than a perfectly crisp one. Fingers are positioned so close to the camera that they obscure the top and bottom of the frame, leaving only a narrow horizontal strip visible in the center. Within this central band, the young woman's eyes are visible; a few strands of hair fall across herface, revealing only her brows and eyes. Her expression is sultry and bold, with a dreamy, heavy-lidded quality and long, fluffy lashes. Her brows are slightly lowered yet retain their arch. She has a feline gaze—sultry and daring. The photo is very dark, featuring deep black shadows and slight grain; it is unretouched, showing natural skin texture.
