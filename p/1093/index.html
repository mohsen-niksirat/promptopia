پرامپت فارسی

Convert the uploaded photo into a cute premium scrapbook-style sticker illustration. Keep the exact face and identity. Place the character as a large white-outline sticker on a notebook paper background with paper clips, tape, and journal aesthetics. Add beautiful handwritten Persian words around the character that are automatically generated based on the person’s appearance, mood, outfit, and pose in the photo. Create matching doodles for each word (stars, hearts, sparkles, flowers, arrows, books, camera, coffee, butterflies, etc.) relevant to the photo.
