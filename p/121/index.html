واترمارک نور طلاییA fashion magazine swimwear feature, waist-up, Fujifilm GFX

پرامپت فارسی

A fashion magazine swimwear feature, waist-up, Fujifilm GFX 100S and 110mm f/2.0, high angle looking down. Resort poolside lounge at dusk — the palm fronds overhead catching the last warm light of the day, the pool surface in the background beginning to reflect the evening sky, the lounger in the warm-to-cool transitional light of the last hour. A 25-year-old Japanese model reclines — authentic fashion editorial photography, a natural candid moment of end-of-day rest. East Asian young woman in her early 20s. Almond-shaped eyes gently closed, the delicate double eyelid structure visible in repose. Straight refined nose. Skin tone fair light beige, the warm remaining sunset light filtering through the palms casting long amber-tinged streaks across her — skin subsurface scattering visible in the warm directional dusk quality, specular micro-highlights with an amber warmth on the nose bridge and cheekbones, fine foundation powder grain perceptible in the transition areas between light and shadow. Naturally straight black hair spread loose against the lounger cushion. She wears the deep burgundy halter-neck one-piece with a soft sheen — in the warm amber dusk light the burgundy takes on a richer red-brown quality, the subtle sheen catching the low directional sun in warm glints across the fabric. Her right arm rests behind her head, left arm relaxed along her side. Eyes closed, expression entirely released. Two or three stray hairs fall across her cheek and temple, natural unplanned imperfection, not geometrically symmetrical. Warm low-angle dusk light filtering through the palm fronds, creating long directional shadows rather than the midday dappled pattern. The mood is warm, luxuriously tired, end-of-day. Subtle ISO 400 film grain in shadow areas, photographic noise texture not CG render smoothness. Kodak Gold 200 — rich amber dusk, warm burgundy, golden palm light. Aspect ratio 2:3. No watermark, no text overlay, not cartoon, not digitally painted, not illustration, not anime.
