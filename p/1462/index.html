Use the uploaded image as the identity reference and preserve the person’s exact facial structure, hairstyle, expression, skin tone, body proportions, eye shape, lips, nose, jawline, and overall identity with extremely high consistency. Transform the portrait into a luxury editorial photograph illuminated only by natural golden-hour window light. Create dramatic chiaroscuro lighting with a single narrow beam of sunlight crossing the face while the rest of the scene fades into deep cinematic shadow. Keep the lighting physically accurate with realistic light falloff, soft ambient bounce, and rich shadow depth. Emphasize natural skin texture, visible pores, subtle imperfections, glossy highlights, and premium beauty retouching without creating plastic skin. Dress the subject in elegant dark clothing such as a black sweater, oversized knit, blazer, or minimalist fashion piece unless the original clothing should remain unchanged. Place the subject beside a textured wall, large window, or minimalist interior with warm sunlight entering from one direction. Keep the background simple, dark, softly blurred, and distraction-free. Capture the image using an 85mm full-frame portrait lens at f/1.8 with shallow depth of field. Apply soft cinematic film grain, matte color grading, deep blacks, warm highlights, and luxury editorial contrast.
