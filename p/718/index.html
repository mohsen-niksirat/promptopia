A cinematic street portrait of a young woman walking through a crowded city, with the subject perfectly sharp while the surrounding pedestrians are blurred using a dramatic panning motion effect. The entire image is monochrome black and white except for the woman's deep navy-blue (or customizable) wool coat, which remains the only vivid color in the scene. She glances back over her shoulder with a calm, thoughtful expression, loose wind-swept hair, soft natural makeup, and a minimalist shoulder bag. The busy urban background is filled with fast-moving commuters rendered as ghostly motion blur, emphasizing loneliness amidst the crowd.
