Strict Composition & Absurdist Claymation Transfer: A nigh-detail 3D reimagining that preserves the EXACT spatial layout, object positioning, and structural composition of the provided reference image. Do not nove or remove elements; transform them into grotesque, humorous clay world. Style: Extreme Aardman-inspired caricature with an absurdist twist. Every character must look hilariously startled or over-enthusiastic. Texture & Messy Aesthetic: Everything is rendered as heavy, matte plasticine.
