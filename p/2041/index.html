پرامپت فارسی

FOOD: [اسم غذا رو وارد کن] The food above is the only input. Do not ask for anything else. Create exactly ONE standalone hand-drawn observation-map illustration of this Persian dish on clean white paper. First understand the dish and automatically determine its correct Persian name, authentic ingredients, preparation method, cooking tools, traditional serving style, accompaniments, garnishes, flavors, aromas, textures, colors, and culturally relevant details. Everything shown must genuinely belong to this specific dish. Never add generic or irrelevant Persian ingredients. Place one large, beautiful, recognizable illustration of the dish in the center or upper-middle. Around it, loosely draw relevant ingredients, spices, herbs, garnishes, cooking tools, serving objects, preparation details, texture close-ups, tiny diagrams, steam, crumbs, cooking marks, and curved arrows. Adapt everything to the actual dish: - Grilled → correct char, skewers, smoke - Stew → correct sauce, consistency and ingredients - Rice-based → correct rice, toppings, saffron or tahdig only when appropriate - Bread/soup/fried/baked → show its authentic preparation and characteristics Use a relaxed Iranian culinary-sketchbook aesthetic: ink and watercolor, hand-painted food study, naturally imperfect outlines, subtle paper texture, faded ink, watercolor absorption, and gentle scanner softness. Keep plenty of white space. Use an organic, irregular layout with staggered drawings and handwritten notes. No grid, collage, panels, borders, symmetry, UI elements, or photorealistic/3D rendering. ALL visible text must be in correct Persian RTL handwriting. Use the Persian name of the dish as the main title and add short handwritten notes about its ingredients, taste, aroma, texture, preparation, and serving. Do not show the English name. Use only culturally appropriate Iranian objects and accompaniments genuinely associated with the dish. No flags or generic Persian decorations. Derive the watercolor palette from the actual dish and its ingredients. Keep it warm, natural, appetizing, slightly bright, and mostly white. FINAL IMAGE: intimate, playful, nostalgic, informative, handmade, appetizing, and unmistakably Persian.
