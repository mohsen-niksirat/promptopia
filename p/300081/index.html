پرامپت فارسی

Create a very professional, modern, and minimalist infographic in Persian that looks like a “personality analysis and facial first impression assessment report.” Place the person’s photo in the center of the page. Around the photo, use lines and arrows to identify different facial features (eyes, eyebrows, face shape, jawline, lips, nose, head angle, gaze, and symmetry), and write a short explanation for each section describing what first impression this feature creates. On the right side of the page, place a panel with rating bars (0–10) including: • Visual calmness • Apparent self-confidence • Determination in gaze • Emotional control • Sociability (first impression) • Formal appearance • Facial friendliness • Charisma • Presence and impact • Trustworthiness in the gaze • Attention to detail (first impression) • Overall facial harmony At the bottom of the page, display an overall score: “Overall First Impression: 7.8 / 10” Next to it, write a 4–5 line analytical summary. At the bottom right, add a section titled “Potential Strengths” including: • Calm appearance • Focused gaze • Noticeable presence • Balanced facial features • Dignity At the bottom left, add a section titled “Areas for Improvement in First Impression” including: • More smiling • Warmer eye contact • Clothing color selection • More open body language • Friendlier posture At the bottom of the page, add a section titled “Communication Suggestions” with several short recommendations for creating a better first impression. The design should look exactly like a professional psychology and facial assessment report: White background Blue and gray color palette Minimal icons Modern Persian-style typography Clean layout Guideline lines over the face Formal and professional report appearance At the bottom of the page, write in small font: “This report is only based on the initial impression from visible facial features and does not determine a person’s actual personality.”
