پرامپت فارسی

A romantic wedding portrait of a bride and groom. The bride is in a white bridal gown richly decorated with pearls and beads, wearing gold jewelry, headpiece, and jasmine flowers in her hair, holding a large elegant white and green bouquet. The groom is in a dark blue suit with tie and boutonnière. Keep the outfits and colors exactly the same. pose: the couple is standing close, facing each other lovingly, smiling with soft eye contact. The background is softly blurred greenery with natural light, in elegant professional wedding photography style.
