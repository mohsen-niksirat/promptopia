پرامپت فارسی

Create a grungy, 3D embossed-style, realistic black-and-white close-up face portrait of a charismatic male pop star. The man should have a strong, masculine, confident appearance, with clearly defined facial structure, natural masculine features, and realistically textured hair. The entire portrait must be constructed as a chaotic mixed-media collage using torn fragments of realistic vintage newspaper clippings, old advertisements with visible halftone dot patterns, ripped typography, and heavily distressed paper textures. Use a strictly black-and-white monochrome color palette, with deep blacks, dirty whites, and multiple shades of gray. Apply these elements in heavy, overlapping layers that naturally sculpt and define the man’s face, jawline, eyes, facial shadows, and hair. Add dynamic street-art elements throughout the composition, including aggressive black ink splatters, spray-paint drips, rough brush strokes, scratches, ripped-paper edges, stencil artifacts, and distressed print imperfections. Integrate fragments of newspaper typography directly into the portrait. Some text fragments—such as his famous song titles, memorable quotes, name, or short phrases associated with him—should remain partially legible and naturally embedded within the collage layers. The background must be completely covered with overlapping vintage newspaper clippings, aged advertisements, torn paper, typography fragments, and halftone textures, seamlessly blending into the portrait. Give the artwork a subtle 3D embossed and layered-paper effect, creating realistic depth between the face, typography, torn newspaper pieces, and background. The overall aesthetic should feel raw, masculine, rebellious, underground, textured, edgy, and premium, combining realistic portrait photography with punk editorial design, vintage printmaking, and contemporary street art. Composition: extreme close-up portrait, face dominant in frame Style: photorealistic mixed-media collage, 3D embossed paper, vintage punk editorial, street art Color: strictly black and white Aspect ratio: 3:4 Name: [اینجا اسم رو وارد کنید]

Text fragments from the newspapers (like his famous song title or his quote) should be legible within the colored sections. The background is vintage newspaper clippings.
