پرامپت فارسی

Use the uploaded photo as the main reference and create one final image in a vertical 4:5 aspect ratio. Carefully analyze the person’s face and preserve their identity exactly. Do not change the facial structure or features in any way. Preserve the same face shape, skin tone, eyes, eyebrows, nose, lips, expression, and overall natural appearance. The person must remain clearly recognizable in all outputs. Only apply different makeup styles. Create one single final image as a 3×2 collage (6 panels total) showing the same person in all panels. Main instruction Do not use fixed generic makeup looks. First analyze the face, then choose 6 makeup styles that are specifically flattering for this person, based on: face shape skin tone and undertone eye shape eyebrow shape lip shape overall vibe and elegance of the face Each of the 6 looks must feel personalized, realistic, soft, and naturally flattering. Important makeup direction All 6 looks should stay in the range of: light makeup soft natural beauty makeup clean girl / polished natural makeup subtle feminine makeup fresh everyday makeup soft elegant makeup Avoid heavy glam, dramatic transformations, overly bold lipstick, very dark smoky eyes, exaggerated contouring, heavy lashes, or anything that makes the face look too edited or artificial. The makeup differences should be visible, but still look natural, wearable, refined, and beauty-enhancing. Layout Show 6 different makeup looks in one final image Arrange them in a clean 3 columns × 2 rows grid Keep the same pose and identity consistent across all 6 panels Background should stay simple and visually consistent Focus on makeup variation only, not face transformation Makeup variety The 6 looks should all be natural-style looks, but with subtle variety, such as: a very natural no-makeup makeup look a soft rosy feminine look a fresh peachy natural look a warm nude polished look a soft elegant formal natural look a slightly defined but still natural chic look The exact colors and makeup choices should be selected based on the face analysis, so the result feels customized for this specific person. Text on image In each panel, write a short Persian name for the makeup style. The Persian text must be: clear elegant readable accurate without spelling mistakes Use short, attractive Persian names that match the soft natural makeup styles. Style Make the final image look like a professional beauty consultation board or premium natural makeup recommendation sheet. Use flattering, realistic lighting and polished beauty-editorial styling. Restrictions Do not change the person into someone else Do not alter facial proportions Do not over-edit the skin unnaturally Do not modify the face beyond makeup Keep the result realistic, beautiful, soft, natural, and personalized
