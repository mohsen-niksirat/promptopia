پرامپت فارسی

A woman (use uploaded photo as face, hair reference), seen from the side (profile view), stands in front of a large mirror indoors. She holds a small two-layer naked cake with "22" candle numbers on top, both candles lit with a warm flame. In the mirror's reflection, instead of her own adult face, a younger version of herself appears as a child aged 5 — same facial structure, same nose, same long brown hair, same eyes, same smile — clearly the same person but younger. She wears a dark outfit, same pose, holding an identical small cake with "5" candle. The little girl looks back at the woman with a soft smile. The adult woman is also smiling warmly toward the reflection. The adult woman does NOT appear in the mirror — only her childhood self is reflected. Written on the mirror glass in casual handwritten black marker: "birthday girl" The mirror has a thin metal frame. Background is softly blurred — warm indoor tones, curtain visible. Lighting is a mix of harsh camera flash from the front creating bright overexposed highlights on skin and surfaces, combined with soft warm candlelight glow from the cake candles. Flash creates slight lens flare and washes out parts of the background. Film photography aesthetic — heavy grain, slight color fade, warm vintage tones, slight vignette, flash overexposure on skin. Vertical 3:4 format. Candid, emotional, nostalgic mood. Shot on 35mm film.
