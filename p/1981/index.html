موتور سواری با پارتنرتA realistic nighttime selfie of two people on a motorcycle

پرامپتوپیا · گالری پرامپت‌های آماده هوش مصنوعی

پرامپت فارسی

A realistic nighttime selfie of two people on a motorcycle or scooter, taken from a close-up, slightly elevated angle using the front-facing camera. In the foreground on the left is the driver, wearing a black motorcycle helmet with a clear visor, gray and white graphic accents. Only the eyes, thick eyebrows, and the upper part of the face are visible; the expression is calm and confident, with the gaze directed at the camera. Behind and to the right sits the second person, leaning slightly towards the driver and embracing them around the chest. They are wearing a black, full-face motorcycle helmet with a raised or clear visor, revealing the eyes, eyebrows, and part of the face. The expression is soft and direct, looking at the camera. Both are wearing dark motorcycle gear and black jackets. They have black protective gloves with hard padding on their hands. The passenger's hands are resting on the driver's chest, and a neat manicure with long, light-colored nails and small dark details is visible. Above the faces, in the center of the image, is a small decorative symbol: a red heart with a white stripe next to an infinity symbol, resembling a sticker from a story. The composition is tight, intimate, and vertical. The faces and helmets occupy almost the entire frame, and the background fades into deep darkness. Barely visible behind them are silhouettes of a street, dark trees or bushes, and blurred, faint lights to the right, along with a gray-green surface of a road or sidewalk. The surroundings are almost completely consumed by the night. The lighting is harsh, from the phone's flash or a bright front light, creating sharp reflections on the glossy helmets, gloves, and jackets, while the background remains very dark with strong contrast. The color palette is predominantly black, graphite, gray, and white, with a small red accent from the heart and barely noticeable warm lights in the distance. The style should resemble a real mobile phone photo taken at night: slightly grainy, high contrast, direct flash lighting, natural proportions, a slight softness and noise, a vertical 4:5 aspect ratio, and a feeling of a spontaneous, romantic motorcycle selfie without any studio staging.
