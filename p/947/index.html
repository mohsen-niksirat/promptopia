Ultra realistic cinematic countryside photography, shot on Canon PowerShot G7X Mark III, authentic real life outdoor photo. STRICT REFERENCE LOCK 1:1: Recreate the same pose, outfit, hairstyle, camera angle, framing, lighting, weather and atmosphere from the reference image. CLIENT IDENTITY RULE: Use client photo only for: - exact face identity - facial features - natural hair color - real hair length and texture Do NOT copy client: - pose - outfit - background - lighting REFERENCE CONTROLS EVERYTHING ELSE. FRAMING: Almost full body photo. Camera placed slightly behind the woman. Vertical frame. Show the whole outfit, legs, boots and dramatic landscape. Woman takes about 50% of the frame. Sky and field must dominate the background. LOCATION: Open countryside field. Golden dry wheat field around her. Small dirt road leading into the distance. Huge dramatic storm clouds covering the sky. Dark blue grey cloudy atmosphere. Before-rain summer mood. POSE LOCK: Woman standing with her back mostly turned to the camera. Body turned slightly sideways. Looking down and to the side over her shoulder. Soft profile view. One leg slightly forward like walking. Natural elegant movement. One hand gently holding the side of the dress. Other hand holding a wicker basket with dried flowers. Same relaxed countryside pose. OUTFIT LOCK: White romantic mini dress. Layered ruffle skirt. Soft lace details. Feminine cottage style. White head scarf / bandana tied around the head. Black tall rubber boots. White socks visible above boots. HAIR: Use client's real hair color. Long hair flowing under the head scarf. Natural wind movement. Loose strands moving from the storm wind. ACCESSORIES: Wicker basket. Dry wild flowers inside the basket. Minimal natural details. LIGHTING: Moody storm daylight. Soft dramatic shadows. Cloud filtered light. Real outdoor contrast. Windy atmosphere. CAMERA STYLE: Canon PowerShot G7X Mark III. Real travel snapshot. Sharp subject. Natural skin texture. Slight grain. Authentic Instagram countryside photo. IMPORTANT: Keep back turned pose. Keep looking down to the side. Keep huge storm clouds. Keep wheat field. Keep dirt road. Keep white dress. Keep head scarf. Keep basket with flowers. Do not make sunny weather. Do not change location. No AI look, no plastic skin, no distorted hands. --ar 3:4 --style raw
