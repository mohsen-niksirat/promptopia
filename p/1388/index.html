پرامپت فارسی

Create a premium, highly detailed Persian infographic titled: "پیامی از نسخه ی ۷۰ ساله ی تو" The user will upload a portrait photo. Transform the uploaded face into a realistic and believable 70-year-old version while preserving the person's identity, facial features, and emotional warmth. If the uploaded person is female, create an elegant elderly woman. If male, create a kind, wise, and warm elderly man. The older version should appear peaceful, healthy, trustworthy, emotionally mature, and full of life experience, with natural aging, expressive eyes, soft wrinkles, and a gentle smile. If previous conversations with the user are available, naturally use that understanding of their personality, values, dreams, concerns, communication style, or recurring themes to personalize the letter. Never invent specific future events or factual predictions. If no previous context exists, write a universally relatable message. The output MUST be a SINGLE premium infographic, not multiple images. Design Style: • Luxury psychology-inspired infographic • Modern editorial magazine layout • Premium visual hierarchy • Beautiful Persian typography • Clean composition • Rich visual storytelling • Minimal but information-rich • Emotionally comforting atmosphere • Elegant premium aesthetic • Soft cinematic lighting • High-end graphic design quality The dominant color palette should use different shades of BLUE combined with white and subtle neutral tones to create feelings of trust, calmness, hope, wisdom, and emotional safety. The infographic should feel modern, luxurious, emotionally intelligent, and highly shareable. Layout should include: • Large realistic portrait of the user's 70-year-old self. • Beautiful Persian title. • A long heartfelt Persian letter. • Small decorative design elements. • Elegant information cards if appropriate. • Balanced spacing. • Premium typography. • Everything contained inside ONE visually stunning infographic. The Persian letter MUST be long enough to feel personal, emotional, and deeply comforting. VERY IMPORTANT: Write completely in natural spoken Persian. Never use formal Persian. Never use literary language. Never sound like an AI. Never sound like a motivational speaker. Never sound like a therapist giving advice. The letter should genuinely feel like the user's own 70-year-old self is finally talking to them after living another 40 years. Speak with warmth, kindness, and unconditional love. Talk exactly like a close friend, an older sibling, or the safest person in someone's life. Use natural spoken Persian expressions like: "میدونی..." "ببین..." "قول میدم..." "همه چی..." "میشه..." "میتونی..." "نگران نباش..." "راستش..." "کم کم..." "یه روزی..." "خودتو اذیت نکن..." "منم یه زمانی دقیقاً همین حسو داشتم..." Do NOT lecture. Do NOT judge. Do NOT give commands. Instead, speak through memories, empathy, reassurance, and gentle reflections. The message should make the reader feel deeply understood. Focus only on universal human experiences such as: • anxiety slowly fading • healing with time • self-forgiveness • hope • resilience • loving yourself • appreciating ordinary moments • protecting your health • accepting uncertainty • inner peace • emotional growth • enjoying life before it passes Avoid mentioning specific future achievements, career success, marriage, wealth, children, or anything unverifiable. The goal is to make the user smile, become emotional, feel safe, and gain hope for tomorrow. The Persian writing must be perfectly readable, grammatically correct, beautifully typeset, and visually balanced. At the bottom of the infographic add an elegant handwritten signature: «با عشق، نسخه ی ۷۰ ساله ی تو 🤍» Also place a very small and elegant credit in one bottom corner:
