مجسمه لاواA surreal, hyper-realistic 3D digital sculpture of the same

پرامپتوپیا · گالری پرامپت‌های آماده هوش مصنوعی

پرامپت فارسی

A surreal, hyper-realistic 3D digital sculpture of the same man from the reference photo — preserve his facial identity, proportions, hairstyle, and skin tone exactly. Only transform the surface texture as described, without altering his real facial structure. His head and upper shoulders are emerging from a smooth white wall, as if seamlessly fused with it. His eyes are gently closed, showing a peaceful, serene expression. His skin appears transformed into cracked volcanic earth, with deep fissures running naturally along the contours of his real face and neck. Inside the cracks, glowing red-orange magma flows intensely, creating a dramatic contrast with the ash-black, cooling-lava outer texture. The cracks start at the forehead, travel across the cheeks, nose bridge, jawline, neck, and reach the top of the chest. The sculpture texture features realistic micro-details: dry stone pores, heat-burned edges, faint ember glow, and molten-light reflections. Lighting is dramatic and cinematic, with soft shadows emphasizing depth and the glowing magma veins. The atmosphere is a blend of photo-realism + fantasy sculpture, with extremely detailed rendering of texture, depth, and light. Art style: hyper-realistic 3D render, digital sculpture, fantasy realism. Aspect ratio: 4:5 or 3:4. NEG: no extra cracks on facial features that distort identity, no plastic skin, no text, no watermark, no deformations.
