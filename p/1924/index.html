A cinematic, portrait of a man sitting outdoors in warm golden-hour sunlight. He has messy hair and a well-groomed beard. He is wearing black rectangular sunglasses and a textured black knit sweater. His pose is relaxed and confident, with one arm bent and resting behind his head, exposing the side of his face and neck. The lighting is strong and directional, casting sharp, dramatic shadows across his face and sweater, emphasizing texture and depth. The sunlight creates a warm glow on his skin while parts of the scene fall into deep shadow for contrast. The background is softly blurred but suggests an outdoor café or terrace setting with glass reflections, wooden structures, and subtle hints of plants. Reflections in glass add visual complexity and a slightly moody, layered look. Style is photorealistic with high contrast, rich shadows, and warm tones. Focus on natural skin texture, realistic fabric detail, and cinematic lighting. Shallow depth of field, subject sharply in focus, background bokeh. Camera feel: Shot on a DSLR or mirrorless camera, 85mm lens, f/1.8, natural light, high dynamic range. Mood: Relaxed, introspective, stylish, slightly mysterious
