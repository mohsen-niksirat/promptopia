چهرت متعلق به کدوم دوره هستشNo beautification or facial changes

پرامپتوپیا · گالری پرامپت‌های آماده هوش مصنوعی

پرامپت فارسی

Analyze the photo and select ONE era: Ancient Persia, Qajar Persia, 1950s Hollywood, 1960s Fashion, Ancient Rome, or Viking Age. Create an ultra-realistic portrait of the SAME person in that era. Preserve identity 100%: face shape, eyes, nose, lips, jawline, age, skin texture, and pores. No beautification or facial changes. Adapt hair to the era while keeping the natural hairline and texture. Use historically accurate clothing, jewelry, accessories, posture, lighting, and background. Women: modest era-appropriate partial head covering (veil/shawl/hat/hood), no deep neckline. Style: cinematic, hyper-realistic, 8K. Add elegant Persian Naskh calligraphy in warm gold: «چهره تو بیشتر متعلق به: [Era]» Include: • 1–2 poetic lines explaining the match • «ویژگی های دوره [Era]:» + 3–5 bullets • «مشاهدات خیلی بامزه درباره تو در [Era]:» + 2–3 witty comments Luxury historical-poster look, refined, nostalgic, clean RTL layout integrated naturally into the portrait.
