پرامپت فارسی

The woman looks directly into the camera, the fingertips of one hand gently touching her chin. The shot is a close-up portrait from the shoulders to the crown of the head, at eye level. The composition is extremely laconic and focuses on the face. The body is turned sideways to the camera, looking into it. A narrow strip of hard light partially crosses the face, dividing the illuminated and darkened halves of the face, creating an expressive play of light and shadow. The main emphasis is on the eyes and face. Focus priority: focus on the face. The skin appears perfectly even and matte. Matte beige-brown eyeshadow is applied to the eyelids with soft blending, and a thin, graphic wing in a rich black shade runs along the lash line. The lashes are long, voluminous, and well-defined. A subtle satin sheen is present at the inner corners of the eyes. The lips are covered with a matte nude lipstick in a warm beige-peach shade (RGB #BE8A6E), the lip contour is defined and natural. A short manicure with a soft oval shape and a translucent milky finish (RGB #F3ECE5) with a glossy finish. Hair is pulled back into a sleek low bun. The model wears a classic, straight-cut jacket made of thick suiting fabric with a matte black finish (RGB #1B1B1B) and a deep V-neck. She wears small hoop earrings made of polished gold-tone metal (RGB #C5A24A). The shoot takes place in a minimalist studio with a plain, light-gray wall without decorative elements. The background remains completely neutral and practically dissolves into soft shadow, focusing attention solely on the model's face. Lighting is a key element of the composition. A single, hard, directional light source is used, positioned slightly to the side of the model. A narrow beam of light crosses the face in a vertical strip, leaving the other half in deep, soft shadow. The contrast is high, with a clear boundary between light and shadow. Illuminated areas of skin have a soft, warm glow, while details are fully preserved without blown-out highlights. The rest of the scene remains muted, enhancing the drama of the shot. Shot with an 85mm portrait lens. Extremely shallow depth of field. Focus on the face. Maximum sharpness is concentrated on the eyes, eyelashes, lips, and skin texture, while the hair, clothing, and background gradually fade into a soft blur. High detail in the skin, jacket fabric, and metal jewelry. Fine art portrait photography, editorial beauty photography, highly detailed, photorealistic.
