Use the uploaded reference image as the exact identity reference. Preserve the person's face with maximum accuracy, including facial structure, eye shape, nose, lips, jawline, skin tone, hairstyle, and overall identity. Do not alter the face, age, or recognizable features. Create a single vertical collage divided into exactly five equal horizontal panels (5-panel storyboard). Every panel must have the exact same height and width, matching the layout of the reference collage. Do not merge or overlap the panels. Maintain the same cinematic aesthetic throughout: soft golden hour / blue hour sunset lighting, muted earthy color grading, shallow depth of field, calm melancholic mood, natural wind-blown hair, oversized burgundy vintage jacket with a light beige collar, round metal glasses, open countryside with a distant horizon, minimalist composition, film photography look, subtle 35mm grain, soft contrast, realistic skin texture, ultra-realistic editorial photography, high detail, natural colors, soft ambient light, no artificial beauty filters. Panel 3: Looking upward while holding a thin twig against the sky. Panel 4: Standing still facing the distant horizon with a relaxed candid posture. Panel 5: Extreme close-up of the eyes and glasses, preserving realistic skin texture and sharp eye detail. Keep the exact identity from the uploaded reference image. Only change the pose, camera angle, framing, and composition. Ensure visual consistency across all five panels with identical color grading, lighting, lens characteristics, and cinematic mood.
