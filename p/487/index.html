Use the reference photo as the absolute identity reference. Preserve the woman’s face and appearance exactly: facial features, face shape, eyes, nose, lips, skin texture, age and unique identity. Do not change identity or facial proportions. Ultra-realistic cinematic vintage portrait, vertical 9:16 composition. A stylish Iranian woman from the 1350s Persian calendar (1970s Iran, Pahlavi era) is standing elegantly in front of the historic Lalehzar Cinema district in Tehran. She is beside a classic vintage red car, with a graceful feminine pose, one hand naturally resting on the car and her body slightly turned to create a beautiful retro fashion silhouette. The composition is a portrait-focused shot. The woman’s face is the absolute main subject. The camera focuses sharply on her eyes, facial details, hairstyle and vintage outfit. Her skin texture, makeup details and expression are captured with extreme realism. The background is very heavily blurred and out of focus: - Lalehzar Cinema facade appears only as a soft nostalgic silhouette - The old cinema entrance, Persian movie posters and the "قیصر" (Qeysar) movie screening board are barely visible through a strong creamy bokeh effect - Vintage Tehran street, old architecture and classic cars appear as soft blurred shapes and colors - No background element should compete with the woman Outfit: Authentic Iranian 1970s fashion style: - Elegant short A-line skirt above the knee with realistic fabric texture - Stylish fitted blouse inspired by Tehran fashion of the 1350s - Thin vintage belt with metallic buckle - Elegant retro high heels - Feminine, luxurious and authentic Pahlavi-era fashion Hair and makeup: - Authentic 1970s Iranian hairstyle with voluminous soft waves and elegant curls - Classic retro blowout with natural volume - Vintage makeup: defined eyeliner, long eyelashes, soft blush, warm lipstick - Realistic skin pores and natural facial texture, no plastic appearance Lighting: Warm golden afternoon sunlight falling softly on her face. Cinematic portrait lighting, gentle highlights, soft shadows, natural skin glow. Vintage realism: The image should look like a real 1970s Tehran fashion photograph captured on analog film, not a digital or AI image. Authentic film details: - Real 35mm analog camera look - Kodak vintage film color palette - Subtle natural film grain - Slightly faded colors - Soft contrast - Realistic lens imperfections - Authentic aged photograph feeling Photography style: Luxury vintage portrait photography, cinematic fashion editorial, 85mm lens, f/1.4, extremely shallow depth of field, sharp focus on woman’s face, creamy background bokeh, realistic skin texture, detailed hair strands, authentic fabric details, photorealistic, ultra detailed, 8K.
