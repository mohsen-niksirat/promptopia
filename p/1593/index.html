عکساتو گوگولی کن.. the subject’s identity, the composition, the lighting

پرامپت فارسی

Take the uploaded image as your foundation and keep it intact... the subject’s identity, the composition, the lighting all stay exactly as they are. Nothing about the main subject should be reshaped or restructured. Now layer in playful, hand-drawn doodles that actually engage with what’s happening in the photo. The doodles should react to the subject... tracing poses, stretching out from limbs, adding little motion streaks, or inventing whimsical elements that play off the energy of the image. Think of them as drawn ON the photo with purpose, not floating randomly on top. Style-wise, keep it loose and sketchy. Organic lines. Slightly wobbly strokes. That casual, illustrated, “drew this in my notebook” feel. Sprinkle in handwritten text around the image too. The words should match the vibe of the scene, feel spontaneous, and lean playful or funny. Don’t default to generic phrases... let each caption be specific to what’s actually happening in THIS image. Context-aware, clever, with personality. No more than 3 phrases, but less is ok too if it fits the mood. Balance is everything. Doodles enhance, never overwhelm. Keep the final result fun, expressive, and ready to post. High resolution, clean overlay, colors vibrant but still natural. Add personification to inanimate objects such as eyes, blush, smile, arms, etc.ص
