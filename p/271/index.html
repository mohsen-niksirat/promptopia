کاپل نور طلاییCouple Cinematic Color

پرامپتوپیا · گالری پرامپت‌های آماده هوش مصنوعی

پرامپت فارسی

Create an ultra-photorealistic cinematic recreation inspired by the viral Empire State Building proposal using the two uploaded reference photos. IDENTITY PRESERVATION (CRITICAL): Use the first reference as the male and the second reference as the female. Preserve their identities with absolute accuracy, including facial structure, skin tone, eye shape, hairstyle, facial hair, body proportions, age, expressions, and all distinguishing features. They must remain instantly recognizable as the people in the reference images. SCENE: The couple stands on the very top observation platform surrounding the Empire State Building antenna at sunrise, over 1,400 feet above New York City. The perspective should create a breathtaking sense of scale while remaining believable and physically accurate. The male is kneeling on one knee, holding an open engagement ring box with a brilliant diamond catching the morning sunlight. His expression is filled with genuine anticipation and emotion. The female stands in front of him with tears of joy, both hands covering her mouth in surprise. Her expression captures disbelief, happiness, and overwhelming emotion. Behind them, a large white fabric banner waves naturally in the wind, reading: "When the power of love beats the love of power, the world knows peace." The fabric should have realistic folds, stitching, translucency, and dynamic movement. ENVIRONMENT: Golden sunrise over Manhattan with warm orange and pink skies. The entire New York City skyline stretches to the horizon. The Empire State Building structure is highly detailed with realistic steel textures, bolts, cables, and antenna components. Soft atmospheric haze creates incredible depth. Morning light reflects from thousands of skyscraper windows. Clouds drift below the couple, enhancing the sense of height. CINEMATIC DETAILS: The engagement ring sparkles with realistic lens reflections. Wind gently moves their clothing and hair. A flock of birds flies across the background. Small helicopters can be seen in the far distance, emphasizing the enormous scale. Subtle floating dust particles catch the sunlight. Natural depth of field keeps the couple perfectly sharp while the distant city gradually softens. WARDROBE: Modern urban climbing attire with premium outdoor clothing, climbing harnesses, ropes, gloves, helmets attached to belts or backpacks, realistic equipment, practical footwear, and weathered textures. Clothing should look authentic and functional rather than stylized. LIGHTING: Golden-hour cinematic lighting. Volumetric sun rays. Global illumination. Natural reflections. Physically accurate shadows. HDR exposure. Film-quality color grading. CAMERA: Shot on an ARRI Alexa 65 with an 85mm anamorphic lens. Low-angle cinematic perspective. Ultra-high dynamic range. Natural perspective with no distortion. Shallow depth of field. Blockbuster movie composition. STYLE: Hyper-realistic photography, National Geographic meets Hollywood blockbuster, ultra-detailed skin pores, realistic hair strands, authentic fabric textures, physically accurate lighting, cinematic realism, 8K, HDR, masterpiece quality, extremely sharp focus, award-winning photography, emotionally powerful, believable, no CGI appearance, no cartoon styling. Aspect Ratio: 4:5
