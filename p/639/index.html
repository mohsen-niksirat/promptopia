Ultra-cinematic intimate portrait based entirely on the uploaded reference image. Use the uploaded photo as the ONLY identity source and preserve the subject's face with maximum fidelity. IDENTITY LOCK (Highest Priority): Maintain the EXACT SAME FACE as the reference image. Preserve identical facial proportions, bone structure, jawline, cheeks, forehead, eyebrows, eye shape and spacing, eyelids, eyelashes, nose shape, lips, ears, hairstyle, hairline, hair texture, skin tone, facial details, expression characteristics, age, ethnicity, and overall identity. Do NOT beautify, stylize, age, de-age, feminize, masculinize, or reinterpret the face. The final result must instantly be recognizable as the same real person. SCENE: A quiet late-night indoor environment beside a warm incandescent vanity lamp. The subject sits close to a mirror, resting the head gently on one hand while gazing softly downward with a peaceful, introspective expression. The mirror creates a natural reflection showing both the real subject and reflected face in a balanced composition. The atmosphere feels calm, nostalgic, cinematic, and emotionally intimate. LIGHTING: Single warm tungsten practical light (approximately 2700K), soft falloff, deep shadows, natural contrast, subtle halation around highlights, glowing bloom, realistic skin illumination, warm amber color palette, gentle rim light from the lamp, no artificial studio look. CAMERA: 85mm full-frame lens, f/1.4, close portrait framing, eye-level angle, shallow depth of field, creamy bokeh, cinematic composition, authentic optical imperfections, soft lens bloom, subtle diffusion filter, realistic highlight roll-off. MOTION: Slight handheld motion combined with slow shutter drag creating delicate motion blur around the moving hand and foreground only. Keep the face and mirror reflection naturally sharp while preserving realistic movement elsewhere. COLOR GRADING: Rich amber highlights, deep brown shadows, cinematic blacks, Kodak Vision3 inspired film color science, subtle film grain, soft contrast, natural skin tones, slight glow, organic texture, no HDR, no oversharpening, no plastic skin, no AI-generated look. QUALITY: Hyper-realistic photography, premium editorial portrait, emotional storytelling, realistic optical rendering, fine skin texture, authentic reflections, physically accurate lighting, ultra-detailed, masterpiece, 8K.
