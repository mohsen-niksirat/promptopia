One hand is raised toward her face, with her fingers elegantly touching her cheek and lips, creating the feeling of a spontaneous selfie pose. She has long eyelashes with a pronounced fox-eye effect. Her nails are almond-shaped.She is wearing a delicate light pink spaghetti-strap top. The lighting is soft and backlit, with the bright sun hidden behind the clouds, creating a milky glow around her head, muted facial shadows, subtle lens flare, visible film grain, and low contrast. Use a cool bluish-gray color palette balanced with warm, natural skin tones.In the bottom-left corner, add a vertical yellow digital date stamp reading 10.08.2026, resembling the timestamp from an old compact camera. Preserve the raw, glamorous summer snapshot aesthetic of the early 2000s.Aspect ratio: 9:16.Use only my uploaded photo as the identity reference.
