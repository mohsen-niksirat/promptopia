پرامپت فارسی

A cinematic 35mm photograph of a stunningly beautiful young woman walking toward the camera through a rainy city crosswalk, low angle shot. She is surrounded by a crowd of men in dark suits with black umbrellas, but she holds the only vibrant red umbrella with a gold handle. She has a sharp black bob with blunt bangs, pale skin, and an intense, fearless gaze. She wears a sophisticated black strapless tailored cocktail dress with an elegant hourglass silhouette, featuring ruffle details and lace trim. Layered pearl choker and draped pearls on her collarbone. Rain droplets on her shoulders and wet strands of hair. The background crowd is blurred and desaturated, creating a sharp focus on her. Wet pavement reflecting the grey sky. Mood: Yakuza noir film, Kodak Vision3 500T style, high contrast, deep blue shadows, the red umbrella as the primary color pop. --ar 9:16
