پرامپت فارسی

I am standing outdoors in an urban environment, captured from a low angle shot that makes me appear taller and emphasizes the buildings rising behind me. My body is positioned sideways to the camera, at a slight angle within the frame, with a relaxed but confident posture. My hands are placed casually in the pockets of my trousers. My head is turned slightly to the side as I look past the camera with a composed, serious, and introspective expression. My shoulders remain relaxed, and my stance communicates calm confidence without exaggerated gestures. I am wearing a loose, textured black knit cardigan layered over a plain white T-shirt. The cardigan has dropped shoulders, wide sleeves, and a casual oversized fit, creating a soft, comfortable silhouette. The T-shirt underneath is simple and fitted at the neckline, contrasting subtly with the heavier knit texture. My trousers are light-colored, straight-cut, and minimalist, completing the clean, neutral-toned outfit. No accessories are visible, keeping the look understated and modern. The camera perspective is distinctly low angle, with a portrait-style focal length around 85mm, compressing the background slightly while maintaining cinematic proportions. The lighting is natural daylight, diffused evenly across the scene, with soft highlights on my face and cardigan. The background features tall city buildings with glass and stone facades, slightly out of focus, adding depth while ensuring full attention remains on me as the subject. The overall atmosphere is cinematic, editorial, and metropolitan, combining minimalist fashion with an urban architectural backdrop.
