پرامپت فارسی

Ultra-realistic professional portrait photograph of the same person from the reference image. Preserve the exact facial identity, facial structure, eyes, eyebrows, nose shape, lips, jawline, skin tone, and natural hair texture. Do not change the person's face. Composition: chest-up portrait, slightly three-quarter angle but still looking directly at the camera with a calm, confident, masculine expression. Pose: subject holding black sunglasses by one temple arm. The sunglasses arm is lightly placed between the slightly parted lips at the corner of the mouth, creating a natural stylish pose. Lips should be slightly open with a subtle relaxed expression, not fully closed. The hand should be raised naturally near the mouth, fingers relaxed and elegant. A silver metal wristwatch must be clearly visible on the same wrist. Wardrobe: crisp white linen button-up shirt, top buttons undone, relaxed open collar, sleeves rolled slightly, elegant minimal style. Background: luxury outdoor architectural setting with warm beige stone arches, soft blurred background, shallow depth of field, creamy cinematic bokeh. Lighting: warm golden-hour natural light, soft directional lighting from the side/front, realistic shadows, natural skin tones. Skin details: ultra-realistic skin texture, visible pores, natural facial details, no plastic skin, no artificial beauty filter. Hair: preserve original hairstyle and hair color from reference, realistic individual hair strands, natural volume. Camera: full-frame DSLR, 85mm lens, f/1.8 aperture, sharp focus on eyes and face, cinematic editorial fashion photography, vertical 9:16 composition, 8K photorealistic quality. Important: The sunglasses pose must look natural and fashionable. Do not place the sunglasses awkwardly on the lips, do not close the mouth, do not create a forced expression.
