پرامپت فارسی

SUBJECT Use the same person as the reference image, identity preserved, recognizable facial features and natural facial proportions. Create a close-up Y2K nightlife portrait with a mysterious, playful expression, head slightly tilted, eyes looking at the camera or just to the side, mouth relaxed, pale flash-lit face, messy after-party energy. The subject should look like a stylish nocturnal club figure, vampire-coded but not fantasy cosplay. [STYLING] Neutral indie sleaze streetwear: black tank top or fitted dark t-shirt, open black shirt or worn leather-like jacket, layered silver necklaces, simple rings, belt, low-rise denim or dark trousers. Hair slightly messy or damp from the club. Styling should adapt naturally to the person and avoid forcing a masculine or feminine silhouette. [COMPOSITION] Vertical 4:5 portrait, close chest-up or waist-up framing, old digital camera selfie-adjacent angle without a visible phone. Slightly off-center composition, cramped club bathroom or stairwell, dirty mirror edge, tiled wall, scratched metal, sticker-covered surface, dark doorway behind the subject. Background should be blurred but still recognizable as nightlife interior. [RENDER STYLE] 2007 MySpace profile photo, indie sleaze club photography, old compact digital camera, harsh flash, high contrast, low fidelity internet image, dirty nightlife editorial, imperfect candid fashion shot. [LIGHTING] Harsh direct flash on the face and chest, overexposed whites, deep black shadows, cold green-blue bathroom light, slight red tint in the shadows, shiny highlights on jewelry and hair. Flash should feel cheap and intense. [COLOR PALETTE] Black, cold white, dirty blue, sickly green, deep burgundy shadows, desaturated skin, faded red accents, no clean pastel colors. [IMAGE DEGRADATION] Digital noise, JPEG artifacts, dirty lens, motion blur at the edges, slight red-eye feeling, crushed shadows, blown highlights, chromatic aberration, dusty frame, low-resolution social media upload texture. [ATMOSPHERE] Dark, sexy without being explicit, playful, nocturnal, messy, nostalgic, underground, early social media profile photo energy, lost MySpace archive, club bathroom after midnight. [AVOID] Changing the reference identity, changing facial proportions, literal vampire fangs, blood, fantasy costume, over-defining gender, gender transformation, polished modern nightclub photo, luxury campaign, clean studio background, outdoor daylight, warm golden light, perfect skin retouching, readable text, logos, watermark, ultra sharp details, distorted face, blurry eyes.
