پرامپت فارسی

A high fashion mixed media editorial portrait that blends realistic fashion photography with expressive hand drawn sketch art. The composition features a confident young woman standing in a three quarter pose, leaning slightly backward with one hand resting naturally by her side and her head turned over her shoulder. She looks away from the camera with a calm, confident, slightly mysterious expression. Long wavy dark brown hair flows naturally across her shoulders, with large silver hoop earrings adding a modern street fashion touch. She wears an oversized white sweatshirt with loose sleeves tucked slightly into high waisted faded charcoal gray straight leg jeans. The jeans feature a wide black leather belt with oversized silver eyelets and a large silver buckle, creating a bold urban aesthetic. The background is a clean white studio backdrop with a subtle light gray rectangular panel centered behind the subject to create depth and a gallery style presentation. The artistic concept combines photography with sketch illustration. Only the left half of the woman's body is fully photorealistic, while the right half transitions seamlessly into a vibrant red line art illustration. The sketch perfectly continues the woman's pose and clothing before expanding into an illustrated companion character standing back to back with her. The illustrated character wears a loose hoodie, wide leg pants, gloves, and a tilted bucket hat. The character has exaggerated spiral eyes, a sharp toothy grin, graffiti inspired styling, and holds two fingers raised near the face in a playful gesture. The entire illustration is created using energetic hand drawn red pencil style outlines with visible sketch strokes and construction lines. Additional red doodle elements surround the composition, including: Large handwritten graffiti lettering across the top reading "VIBIN'" in bold expressive brush style. A vertical decorative panel on the left containing abstract symbols, a large question mark, an "X", spiral icon, and minimalist graphic marks. Floating cloud doodles behind the subjects. Small stars, crosses, motion marks, and playful graphic accents scattered throughout the artwork. Light sketch overlaps extending over the realistic photograph, creating the illusion that the drawing is being created directly on top of the image. The color palette is intentionally minimal: Pure white background. Neutral grayscale clothing. Natural skin tones. The illustration uses only vivid saturated red line work, making it the strongest visual element. Lighting is soft studio lighting with even illumination, minimal shadows, crisp detail, and clean editorial quality.
