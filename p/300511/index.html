Prompt: اگه تو دنیای جادو بودی، کی میشدی؟ این پرامپت بهت میگهCat Photo Nature

پرامپت فارسی

Analyze the uploaded photo and transform the exact SAME person into a completely original, Hogwarts-inspired wizard character from an unknown magical fantasy universe. Do NOT recreate Harry Potter, Hermione, or any existing characters. The final result must look like a premium, ultra-realistic lost character poster from an unreleased magical fantasy movie (8K cinematic detail, vertical 4:5 aspect ratio). STRICT IDENTITY & ANATOMY PRESERVATION: Use the uploaded photo as the absolute facial foundation. You must strictly preserve the face structure, eye/eyebrow shape, nose, lips, jawline, skin texture, pores, age, ethnicity, and natural facial asymmetry. Do NOT create a generic fantasy face, face-swap appearance, or plastic look. The body must have highly realistic human anatomy, correct head-to-body proportions, and natural posture. Randomize the cinematic facial expression (e.g., confident magical warrior, focused spellcaster, mysterious, or angry during a duel). RANDOMIZATION SYSTEM (EQUAL PROBABILITY): Before generating, randomly select elements from the categories below. Do NOT choose based on the person's real-life appearance. Avoid cliché combinations (e.g., no automatic Ravenclaw + Owl). 1. Hogwarts House (Controls colors/vibe): Gryffindor (crimson/gold, bold, courageous), Hufflepuff (yellow/black, nature-inspired, warm), Ravenclaw (blue/silver, elegant, mysterious), OR Slytherin (green/silver, dark elegant fashion, serpent elements). 2. Magical Role: Elite Spell Duelist, Forbidden Magic Researcher, Ancient Artifact Hunter, Potion Master, Dragon Trainer, Curse Breaker, Time Magic Student, or Secret Guardian. 3. Outfit & Wand: Premium fantasy movie costume (enchanted long coat, magical battle uniform, or ancient scholar gear) with realistic fabric folds. Give them a unique wand (elder, yew, or ancient oak with a rare magical core). 4. Companion & Artifact: Random companion (phoenix, magical fox, black cat, tiny dragon, raven, or rare mythical creature—do not automatically pick an owl) and a legendary item (ancient spell book, cursed ring, time artifact, etc.). 5. Environment: Forbidden underground library, floating wizard city, dragon mountain sanctuary, crystal cave, or abandoned spell chamber (Do NOT always use Hogwarts castle). 6. Pose & Camera (Full body, 3/4, or waist-up): Action (casting spell, dueling), Discovery (opening ancient book), Heroic, or Emotional. Use cinematic camera angles (low-angle hero shot, dramatic side profile, over-the-shoulder) with epic magical lighting (floating candlelight, glowing spell particles, moonlight). PERSIAN FANTASY MOVIE POSTER TYPOGRAPHY: Add elegant Persian fantasy typography to the poster exactly as written below: Main title: «اگر تو در دنیای جادو بودی: [Generate & Insert Character Role in Persian]» Additional text: «خانه هاگوارتز: [Insert House in Persian]» «قدرت ویژه: [Insert Ability in Persian]» «چوب دستی: [Insert Wand type in Persian]» «همراه جادویی: [Insert Companion in Persian]» Include ONE of these dramatic lines: «هر جادوگری یک راز دارد...» OR «سرنوشت بعضی ها با یک طلسم تغییر می کند.» FINAL STYLE: Completely random combinations, ultra-realistic, not cosplay, not a cheap face swap. A complete original magical legend.
