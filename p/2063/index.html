For a single woman, single man, or couple, apply the same transformation to each person individually. Preserve the original hairstyle and recreate it as a miniature collectible doll hairstyle, including its exact overall shape, length, curls, parting, volume, hair color, and visible accessories. Do not add bows, dresses, shoes, jewelry, or accessories that are not present in the reference Place the miniature figurine on an elegant transparent round acrylic display stand, gently held by a realistic human hand visible in the frame. Use a narrow depth of field,warm soft lighting,a cozy minimalist gallery or elegant indoor environment,cinematic background blur, realistic macro photography,natural hand proportions and scale, and subtle reflections on the transparent acrylic base.
