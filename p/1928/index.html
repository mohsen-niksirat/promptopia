پرامپت فارسی

Use the uploaded image as the ONLY reference for the person’s identity. Preserve the person’s exact facial identity, facial features, face shape, skin tone, age, hairstyle, coloring, and natural appearance. Do not beautify or reinterpret the person. Create a photorealistic portrait of the SAME person naturally holding a cat in their arms. IMPORTANT: Before designing the cat, analyze the person’s visible appearance and overall visual characteristics. Create a cat that looks like the person’s true feline counterpart — matching their overall coloring, hair color, eye color, facial expression, mood, distinctive visual traits, and overall vibe. The cat should feel like a natural animal version of THIS SPECIFIC PERSON, not a random cat. The resemblance must be subtle but clearly intentional: similar color palette, eyes, expression, markings, and personality translated naturally into feline features. DO NOT give the cat human facial features, human eyes, human skin, or a human-like face. It must remain 100% anatomically realistic as a cat. Place the cat comfortably and naturally in the person’s arms, close to their chest, with realistic paws, body proportions, fur, anatomy, and interaction. Photorealistic photography, highly detailed skin and fur, natural lighting, realistic shadows, cinematic color grading, soft intimate atmosphere, authentic proportions. Make it look like a real photograph of the person with their feline counterpart.
