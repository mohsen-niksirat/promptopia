Prompt: GirlWoman Portrait Realistic

پرامپت فارسی

Create a high-skill graphite pencil drawing of a reference woman playing the violin, combining realistic anatomy with controlled, expressive sketch rendering. STYLE PRIORITY (CRITICAL) The drawing must feel like it is made by an experienced artist — composed, controlled strokes, not messy or amateur. Loose in some areas, but always intentional and refined. Composition Vertical 4:5 portrait, upper-thigh to head, centered, clean white background. Expression & Mood (CRITICAL) Eyes closed, calm and emotionally immersed. Subtle expression, not dramatic. No smile. Outfit (IMPORTANT) One Piece Skirt Dress. Linework & Rendering (VERY IMPORTANT) Hair: flowing, dynamic strokes with clear direction and rhythm (not random scribbles) Face & skin: smooth tonal shading with minimal visible strokes (soft blending feel) Dress (TOP): dense, rich graphite shading (nearly solid dark mass) Dress (SKIRT): loose but controlled strokes, suggesting motion without chaos Lines must be confident and purposeful, not shaky Anatomy & Pose Natural, slightly asymmetrical pose Hands realistic but cleanly drawn (not overly sketchy) Bow angle natural and elegant Violin Details Clean and precise structure Fine, straight string lines Subtle highlights created through contrast, not rough marks Lighting Soft directional light from upper-left Gentle shadows, smooth transitions Negative Constraints (IMPORTANT) No messy scribbles No rough amateur sketch look No harsh cross-hatching on face No overly polished digital rendering Keywords fine art graphite, controlled sketch, composed strokes, classical drawing, realistic anatomy, elegant rendering
