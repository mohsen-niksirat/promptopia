پرامپت فارسی

Create a cute personalized SCHOOL BOOK LABEL / NOTEBOOK STICKER for a young child using the uploaded photo as the visual reference. FORMAT: Create a clean horizontal rectangular sticker with rounded corners, suitable for printing and sticking on the front cover of a school book or notebook. CHILD CHARACTER: Use the uploaded child's photo as the reference. Transform the child into a charming, high-quality animated character while keeping the child clearly recognizable. Preserve the child's: recognizable facial features hairstyle and hair color skin tone eye color when visible glasses or other recognizable features if present general appearance and personality Render the child in a warm, whimsical hand-painted animated movie style, inspired by cozy Japanese fantasy animation and premium family animation. Do not create a photorealistic portrait. The result should look like a cute illustrated animated version of the same child. Show the child as a friendly half-body or head-and-shoulders character on one side of the label, smiling naturally. SCHOOL THEME: Surround the character with a few small, playful school-themed illustrations such as: pencils, colorful crayons, tiny books, stars, ruler, backpack, paper airplane, numbers, alphabet letters, clouds or small doodles. Keep these decorations minimal and balanced. Do not overcrowd the sticker. INFORMATION AREA: Reserve a large, clean and clearly separated area for the student's information. Include these three editable fields: نام و نام خانوادگی : __________________ درس : cute personalize کلاس :cute personalized SC The blank writing areas must be large enough for a parent or child to write on after printing. Do NOT automatically invent a child's name, class, school or book name. DESIGN: Cute premium children's stationery design Soft pastel colors Warm and cheerful atmosphere Beautiful hand-drawn details Clean composition Rounded shapes Child-friendly typography High readability White or very light background behind writing areas Subtle decorative border Professional printable sticker design Balanced negative space IMPORTANT: The child's portrait should occupy approximately 30–35% of the sticker. The information fields should occupy approximately 55–60% of the sticker. The character must NOT cover the writing fields. Keep all important elements safely away from the edges so the sticker can be cut after printing. No mockup. No book shown behind the label. No hands holding the sticker. No perspective distortion. Show ONLY the finished flat rectangular label on a clean neutral background. High-resolution printable illustration, crisp edges, clean details, professional children's stationery quality.
