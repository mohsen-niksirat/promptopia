پرامپت فارسی

Vertical photorealistic photograph in the aesthetic of warm film macro photography, without text or watermarks. Extremely close-up framing: only the eyes of two people are visible; the faces are not readable or recognizable — the frame contains only the eye area, part of the bridge of the nose, and fragments of the forehead/cheeks, with everything else cropped out. Use the input photos strictly only as identity references; do not change the eye color. Scene and composition: two eyes are positioned diagonally, as if the people are lying next to each other and looking in different directions; one eye is closer to the upper right corner, the other closer to the lower left corner. Very tight cropping, creating an intimate close-up feeling. A slight tilt of the frame so that the eyebrow and eyelid lines form a diagonal composition. Both eyes are looking directly into the camera. Eye appearance: natural eyelashes, soft shadows from the eyelids, clear highlights reflected in the pupils. Eyebrows look natural, with individual hairs visible. Lighting and color: warm, soft side lighting, smooth tonal transitions, no harsh highlights. Film-style color grading: warm beige and caramel midtones, slightly muted colors, soft contrast. Texture and style: pronounced film grain across the entire frame, subtle vignette, barely visible dust particles/scan dots, soft sharpness without excessive digital over-sharpening. Fine details (eyelashes, eyebrows, reflections in the eyes) are in focus, while other areas can be slightly softer. Technical: the feeling of macro/extremely close photography, 50–85mm lens look (or macro lens), shallow depth of field, focus strictly on the irises. High realism, no additional objects, no logos.
