پرامپت فارسی

Photorealistic cinematic photo, 4:5 vertical frame, shot from a very low worm's-eye angle at grass/flower height, wide-angle lens (24–28mm). In the center of the frame, chest-deep, is [SUBJECT: age, gender, skin tone, hair style/color], wearing [CLOTHING: color/type of top, minimal or no visible text], with [ACCESSORY: e.g. thin chain necklace, optional]. Calm, natural pose, body slightly turned to one side, head raised and facing the viewer directly; focus on the upper body and face. Foreground: large blurred yellow bees, yellow wildflowers, and green stems in strong soft-focus, with one large warm orange lens-flare/glare spot at the bottom of the frame. Middle ground: wild plants and flowers surrounding the subject — some stems crisp and in focus, others softly blurred for depth. Background/sky: wide turquoise-blue open sky with large white cumulus clouds, positioned upper-left. Numerous small orange petals, seeds, and leaves drift through the air around the subject at varying distances, some sharp and some motion-blurred, creating a floating-particle effect. Lighting: natural daylight, sun positioned high and slightly behind to the left; soft rim/contour light on hair and shoulders, warm highlights against a cool sky, teal-and-orange color grade, moderate vignette. Style/parameters: shallow depth of field, strong foreground bokeh, tack-sharp subject, cinematic, ultra-realistic, highly detailed, natural film grain, slight atmospheric haze, no text overlays, no logos, no watermark. Negative prompt: illustration, CGI, cartoon, plastic/waxy skin, overexposed highlights, heavy noise, cluttered/dirty background, extra people, buildings, wires, large readable text on clothing, artifacts, warped or deformed hands/arms/shoulders. size 3:4
