پرامپت فارسی

Preserve the target character's real facial identity, face shape, skin tone values in monochrome, hairstyle, hair volume, eyebrows, eyes, nose, lips, jawline, facial hair if present, earrings or accessories if suitable, and natural expression as accurately as possible. The character must keep the same pose as the original image: upper body slightly angled, head tilted to one side, face close to the camera, looking directly toward the camera with a calm, mysterious, serious expression. The face must remain mostly hidden in deep shadow, with only subtle highlights revealing the cheekbone, nose bridge, lips, ear and head outline. Maintain the exact same high-contrast black and white lighting setup: strong bright white background, very dark foreground silhouette, dramatic overhead/back light, extremely deep shadows on the face and neck, soft rim light around the head and shoulders, minimal visible facial details, cinematic editorial contrast. The clothing should be adapted to the target character while keeping the same minimal black oversized crewneck / sweatshirt style. The outfit must remain dark, simple, premium, and almost fully blended into the shadows. Keep the small centered chest graphic/text placement if suitable, but make it subtle and unreadable or replace it with a minimal abstract mark. Use a 50mm to 85mm portrait lens look, close portrait perspective, shallow depth of field, soft film grain, high dynamic range, deep blacks, clean white background, premium fashion photography style. No extra objects, no logos, no readable text, no watermark, no distortion, no cartoon style.
