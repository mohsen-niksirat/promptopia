مدل مو جنگلیGirl Photo Nature

پرامپت فارسی

Take ONLY the face from the source photo: facial features, proportions, natural asymmetry, hair color and texture, skin tone. The face is slightly out of focus, recognizability is preserved through the shape of the face, head silhouette, and hair color. Completely ignore everything else from the source photo: background, clothing, pose, lighting, and composition. Do not transfer any elements from the original image. Create a completely new scene. Inversion of focus. The camera is focused on a small forest lake surrounded by nature. In sharp focus are the calm surface of the water with reflections of trees, green forest, wild grass, small white flowers by the shore, and soft sunlight filtering through the leaves. The main subject of the photograph is the lake and the reflections. In the foreground, the girl is naturally positioned near the water and slightly extends beyond the plane of focus. She does not look like a separate object, but blends into the surrounding environment. Her figure is softly blurred, facial details are not sharp, but the shape of her head, hair, and silhouette remain recognizable. She is sitting on the grass at the edge of the lake, touching the surface of the water with one hand. She is slightly turned away from the camera, caught in a calm private moment. Her facial expression is calm, with a barely noticeable closed-lip smile, without posing. Her hair moves slightly in the light breeze. She is wearing a simple summer outfit: a lightweight top made of natural fabric and blue denim shorts. The composition is imperfect and spontaneous: the girl is positioned slightly to the side of the frame, not in the center. Some of the grass or flowers in front of the camera are slightly blurred. Most of the image is occupied by the lake, reflections, trees, and natural surroundings. Shot handheld on a smartphone, like a spontaneous memory from a trip. Slightly imperfect composition, natural depth of field, imperfect focus, realistic smartphone camera texture. Raw unprocessed photograph, natural skin texture, noticeable camera grain, slight noise, small exposure imperfections, realistic amateur photography. No plastic skin, no beauty retouching, no CGI, no illustration, no drawing, no artificial smoothness. Natural muted colors, no HDR, no filters, no text, no watermarks. –ar 4:5 –raw
