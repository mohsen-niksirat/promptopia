پرامپت فارسی

Using the uploaded photo as the ONLY facial and appearance reference, create an ultra-realistic scene where I am sitting at a cozy street café on a wooden bar stool, casually posing next to a miniature cartoon version of myself. The cartoon character is about half my height, sitting naturally beside me and interacting with me in a cute, friendly way. The miniature character must be a stylized 3D cartoon while preserving my exact identity: same face shape, facial proportions, hairstyle, hair color, eye color, eyebrows, nose, lips, skin tone, expression, and overall recognizable appearance. My real version must remain 100% photorealistic. Both characters must wear the exact same outfit, accessories, hairstyle, and colors as in the uploaded photo. Preserve every important facial detail from the reference image. Keep realistic skin texture, natural pores, fine facial hair, detailed eyelashes, individual hair strands, realistic lighting, authentic fabric textures, and accurate colors. Do not beautify, redesign, or change my facial features. The miniature cartoon should have Pixar-quality 3D styling with slightly larger eyes and head, soft rounded features, and realistic clothing folds, while remaining instantly recognizable as me. Natural candid pose, warm cinematic daylight, shallow depth of field, premium street photography, realistic perspective, highly detailed, DSLR look, 85mm lens, f/2, soft natural shadows, ultra-sharp focus, HDR, 8K, extremely realistic. No split image, no inset photo, no collage, no comparison frame, no text, no watermark, no logo, no extra people, no duplicated body parts, no distorted anatomy, no oversmoothing, no AI-looking skin. Identity preservation is the highest priority. The generated face must match the uploaded photo as closely as possible. Do not reinterpret, redesign, or replace any facial features. The result should immediately be recognizable as the same person.
