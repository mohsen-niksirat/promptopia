پرامپت فارسی

Create an ultra-premium luxury product advertisement using the uploaded product image as the ONLY subject. IMPORTANT PRODUCT RULES: The uploaded product must remain EXACTLY the same. Do NOT redesign it. Do NOT change its shape. Do NOT modify its proportions. Do NOT alter its color. Do NOT replace its material. Do NOT add or remove any parts. Do NOT simplify or stylize it. Do NOT generate another version of the product. Carefully analyze the uploaded product and preserve every visible detail exactly as it is. Place the original uploaded product naturally in the center of the composition on a carved stone pedestal. The environment should be an elegant royal palace inspired by Mughal and Indian architecture with majestic sandstone columns, intricate carvings, golden decorative details, grand arches and luxurious historical atmosphere. A rich deep red velvet fabric flows gracefully over the pedestal and down onto the polished marble floor. Warm golden-hour sunlight streams through the palace arches creating soft volumetric rays, cinematic lighting, subtle haze and elegant highlights. The background contains softly blurred palace structures and distant silhouettes, creating depth while keeping full attention on the product. Composition must be perfectly symmetrical with the product exactly centered. Luxury commercial photography. Museum-quality composition. Ultra realistic. Natural reflections. Photorealistic textures. Premium editorial advertising. Large depth. Shallow depth of field. 85mm lens. f/2.8 Soft cinematic shadows. High-end product photography. Luxury branding aesthetic. Golden ambient lighting. Rich warm color grading. Extremely detailed. Hyper realistic. 8K. No people in foreground. No text. No logo. No typography. No watermark. No extra objects. The uploaded product is the hero object and must remain completely unchanged while only the environment adapts around it.
