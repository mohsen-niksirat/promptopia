اسب سینماییA hyper-realistic cinematic beauty portrait

پرامپتوپیا · گالری پرامپت‌های آماده هوش مصنوعی

پرامپت فارسی

Create a portrait without altering facial features. A hyper-realistic cinematic beauty portrait; a close-up editorial shot featuring dramatic shadow play. Minimal clothing—a strapless top; no jewelry, no props. Extreme close-up; face tilted diagonally across the frame; eyes gazing into the camera with a soft yet intense look; lips slightly parted; hair pulled back into a sleek, high, long ponytail. Flawless makeup; full, matte "dusty rose" lips with a lip liner one shade darker than the lipstick. Photography style: rich, warm amber-orange cinematic tones; a single bright beam of light falls precisely on one half of the face, fully illuminating that side while the other remains in deep shadow (clean split-lighting/chiaroscuro effect); high contrast with deep blacks; a subtle glow/halo on the face; defined edges; very fine film grain; an intimate, moody editorial atmosphere; realistic look. Micro-details of the skin. 9:16 aspect ratio.
