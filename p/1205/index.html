پرامپت فارسی

Analyze my uploaded photo and identify the most visually similar global celebrity (face shape, eyes, nose, lips, jaw, skin, hair, age, overall feel). Preserve my identity exactly — no changes to any facial features. Create a luxury cinematic 8K poster with both of us standing together as if in a professional photoshoot. Use dramatic lighting, elegant background, natural skin detail, and clean composition. Add Persian RTL text on the poster: «شبیه ترین چهره معروف جهانی به تو: [نام]» «شغل: [شغل]» «دلیل شهرت: [توضیح کوتاه]» «نقاط مشترک چهره:» [۴ تا ۶ شباهت ظاهری] All Persian text must be right-aligned, readable, and styled to match the poster design.
