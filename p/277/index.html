پرامپت فارسی

A warm cinematic lifestyle portrait of a young male figure seated at a window table inside a cozy café, holding a plain white ceramic coffee cup with both hands raised to chest level — one hand gripping the cup comfortably. His gaze is directed off to the side toward the window with a soft, contemplative, and slightly wistful expression — as if lost in thought while watching the world outside, lips relaxed with the faintest hint of a gentle smile. He wears a plain white fitted crew-neck t-shirt layered under an open loose-knit camel-brown cardigan with a relaxed drape, sleeves pushed up slightly on the forearms. His hair is medium length, dark with subtle warm highlights, naturally tousled and loosely swept with volume and texture. Light stubble covers the jaw. A second white ceramic cup and saucer sits on the wooden café table in the lower left foreground, slightly out of focus. He is seated beside a large café window — the window frame visible on the left edge of the frame with soft diffused daylight streaming in, creating bright natural backlight and side light. The background is a warmly blurred café interior — warm wooden tones, pendant lights as soft bokeh orbs, blurred rustic décor and furniture suggesting a cozy specialty coffee shop. The overall lighting is bright, airy, and warm — natural window daylight as the key light, soft and flattering with warm interior ambient fill. Color palette: warm camel-brown, clean white, natural wood tones, soft warm bokeh background, bright natural window light. Photography style: warm lifestyle editorial, café portrait, 50mm lens, shallow depth of field background softly blurred, bright airy natural light, photorealistic, 8K.
