پرامپت فارسی

Create an ultra-realistic close-up studio portrait from the uploaded photo. Preserve the person’s identity with maximum accuracy: exact face shape, eyes, nose, lips, jawline, age, skin texture, pores, freckles, fine lines, and natural imperfections. Do not beautify, reshape, or alter facial features. The subject is facing straight toward the camera with a perfectly frontal composition. Expression is serious and neutral: no smile, no frown, relaxed facial muscles, calm eyes, and a natural resting face. The portrait should look like it was taken by a high-end professional camera in a photography studio, with cinematic quality, sharp focus, and realistic skin details. Keep most of the face in soft, dark shadow, as if covered by a dim veil of light. Only the eye area is illuminated by a brighter horizontal rectangular beam of light across both eyes, creating dramatic contrast. Soft sunset-toned highlights and shadows with subtle warm golden-orange hues. Photorealistic, premium editorial portrait, natural colors, extremely detailed, 8K.
