Clean the mirror and its visible surface completely. Remove all stains, smudges, fingerprints, dust, water spots, and unwanted objects or marks from the mirror while preserving realistic reflections. Naturally correct any minor mirror distortion, perspective issues, or uneven edges without making the image look edited. Do NOT change the person's identity, face, facial features, skin texture, body shape, hair, clothes, pose, posture, hands, phone, expression, or reflection. Keep the person and all important details exactly as they appear in the original image. Preserve the original lighting, colors, shadows, depth, composition, camera perspective, and background. Do not add or remove any objects other than the unwanted marks and dirt on the mirror. The final result must look like the original photo was taken with a perfectly clean mirror. Keep it photorealistic, natural, subtle, and free from AI artifacts or over-editing.
