عروسی لاکچریA moody high-fashion editorial portrait of a late-20s man

پرامپتوپیا · گالری پرامپت‌های آماده هوش مصنوعی

پرامپت فارسی

A moody high-fashion editorial portrait of a late-20s man seated at a white marble table, leaning forward with his fist resting near his lips and staring straight into camera, styled as the hero in a tailored light heather-gray double-breasted wool suit with peak lapels, crisp white cotton shirt worn open at the collar, a clean white pocket square, and a minimalist steel wristwatch on a black leather strap; the setting is a dim luxury lounge with towering charcoal drapery, shadowy indoor palms, and an arched warm-lit bar niche behind him, lit by a small brass table lamp and soft ambient spill that sculpts his face with gentle falloff and deep controlled shadows. shot on an 50 mm lens at f 2.8 ISO 640 1 125 s, with the subject tack-sharp and the background softly blurred. Prioritize premium texture realism: natural skin pores with soft matte highlights, subtle lip texture, clean grooming, and slightly glossy dark hair combed back with a loose center part; the suit shows crisp wool weave, sharp structure, and restrained speculars on metal. He is ethnically ambiguous with nationality unspecified, warm olive-tan skin, dark eyes, thick straight brows, a straight nose, full lips, and a defined jaw and cheekbones; athletic, broad-shouldered build with a tall impression, clean-shaven with the faintest stubble. Build lighting with a large soft key from camera-left, negative fill on camera-right for sculpted contrast, a subtle warm rim from the background arch for separation, and tightly controlled practical glints from the lamp. Maintain cinematic exposure by preserving highlight detail on shirt and marble while retaining rich blacks in the curtains and avoiding blown reflections. Compose as a centered half-body portrait from table height, using the lamp and folded dark eyeglasses as quiet foreground anchors and reducing background guests to soft shapes with minimal clutter. Apply a refined editorial grade with deep neutral shadows, gently warm highlights, clean retouching, and subtle film-like grain. The mood is intimate, dark-luxe, confident, and impeccably tailored.
