پرامپت فارسی

Create an ultra-photorealistic transformation of the person in the uploaded reference image, using it as the strict and permanent identity anchor, changing only skin pigmentation to a believable deep-brown complexion with natural warm-to-neutral undertones and transforming the scalp hair into dense, natural black Afro-textured tight curls, while preserving every other aspect of the photograph with maximum fidelity. Keep the exact original facial structure and geometry completely unchanged — the shape, size, position, proportions, and relationships of the eyes, eyelids, eye spacing, eyebrows, nose, nostrils, lips, mouth, forehead, temples, cheeks, cheekbones, ears, chin, jawline, overall facial width, beard and mustache shape and density, facial-hair growth pattern, and hairline must all remain identical to the original. Preserve the exact original expression, gaze direction, apparent age, facial asymmetry, bone structure, and every distinctive identifying characteristic so the person remains instantly recognizable as the same individual. Treat the pigmentation change as recoloring the existing skin surface, not replacing it with a new texture: preserve the underlying facial contours and anatomical detail while adding subtle natural variation across the forehead, temples, cheeks, nose, under-eye area, mouth, jaw, ears, and facial-hair region — avoiding redness, orange tones, oversaturation, or unnaturally uniform color. Give the lips a fuller, more voluminous appearance with a natural darker-brown two-tone pigmentation while keeping their exact original proportions and shape, and preserve the original eyes and eyebrows exactly, adjusting pigmentation only where naturally appropriate. Render the skin as genuine photographed human skin, not digital texture: extremely fine, irregular, naturally varying pores, faint vellus hairs, delicate lines, and subtle imperfections that differ realistically by region — finer on the forehead, slightly more visible on the nose and inner cheeks, smooth and delicate on the eyelids and under-eyes — without covering the whole face in uniform, gritty, waxy, or procedural-looking texture. At normal viewing distance the skin should read as natural, with microtexture only apparent on close inspection, and it must respond to the original lighting with natural highlights, soft specular response, and subtle translucency — no beauty retouching, smoothing, sharpening, or plastic/CGI appearance. For the hair, keep the exact original hairline, hairstyle, silhouette, length, and volume, converting only the strands into naturally irregular individual coils rather than a uniform pattern, and preserve the beard and mustache's exact shape, boundaries, and growth pattern, adjusting only pigmentation or texture as needed for a physically natural result. Do not alter facial anatomy in any way — no wider nose, altered nostrils, changed eye shape or spacing, altered cheekbones, jawline, chin, or facial width, no beautification, de-aging, aging, symmetry correction, or ethnic-feature exaggeration. Preserve the original pose, head orientation, expression, gaze, clothing, accessories, lighting direction and intensity, shadows, highlights, background, depth of field, camera perspective, angle, framing, crop, and composition exactly. The final image must look like an authentic high-resolution photograph captured by a real camera — never a digitally recolored face, CGI render, or AI-generated skin surface.
