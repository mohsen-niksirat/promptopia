Create a cinematic Persian epic poster from the uploaded photo using the same person as the main subject. Preserve facial identity as closely as possible, including face shape, eyes, eyebrows, nose, lips, skin tone, age, hairstyle, expression, and overall likeness. Do not significantly alter the face. Character Selection First, carefully analyze the person's visible facial features, expression, posture, style, presence, and overall aura. Then select the single most suitable character exclusively from Ferdowsi's Shahnameh. Rules: - Consider the full Shahnameh cast (200+ characters), not only famous heroes. - The character may be a king, queen, princess, warrior, prince, noblewoman, sage, messenger, tragic figure, antagonist, or any other authentic Shahnameh character. - Never use characters outside Shahnameh. - Avoid repetitive or generic choices. - The selection must feel highly specific, unique, and visually well-matched to the individual. Visual Design Depict the same person as the chosen Shahnameh character, wearing historically and narratively appropriate clothing, armor, jewelry, crown, symbols, and accessories. Place them in a majestic setting relevant to that character, such as: - Persian royal palace - Imperial court - Ancient Persian garden - Mountain fortress - Mythical landscape - Legendary battlefield - Any Shahnameh-inspired environment connected to the character Art Style - Cinematic - Ultra-realistic - Luxurious - Persian epic fantasy - Dramatic lighting - Highly detailed - Premium movie-poster quality - Social-media-ready - Elegant Persian typography Poster Text (Persian) اگر در شاهنامه بودی، این شخصیت می شدی: [Character Name] Under the name, include: [Role / Title / Defining Trait] Then add a one-line factually accurate biography of the character from Shahnameh. تحلیل چهره تو: Include 3–5 short Persian bullet points describing only visible traits: - Facial structure - Eyes and expression - Style and appearance - Presence and overall vibe Make them personalized and specific, not generic. چرا این شخصیت شدی؟ Write 2–4 short Persian lines explaining why this specific Shahnameh character matches the person's appearance, expression, presence, and aura. The explanation should be thoughtful, character-specific, and non-generic. Poetic Section Add 1–2 Persian poetic lines inspired by the chosen character and the person's overall atmosphere. Final Output A breathtaking luxury Persian fantasy poster with sophisticated layout design, beautiful Persian typography, strong Shahnameh atmosphere, and a unique character match based on the person's visual presence.
