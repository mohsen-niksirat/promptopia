پرامپت فارسی

Transform the person in this photo into a 3:4 frame whimsical "Mini-Me Universe". Create 6–8 tiny, adorable 3D animated mini versions of the main person in the image, each perfectly matching their face, hairstyle, clothing, colors, and overall appearance. The mini characters should be scattered naturally around the scene, interacting playfully with the environment and with the original person. Examples of interactions: • Sitting on shoulders • Climbing on arms, legs, or clothing • Standing on nearby objects • Waving at the camera • Looking up at the main character • Playing together • Mimicking the main person's pose • Hanging from objects • Exploring the scene curiously Maintain strong identity consistency so every mini character is clearly a miniature version of the original person. Style: • High-end Pixar / Disney-quality 3D character design • Cute, expressive facial features • Realistic proportions with slightly oversized heads • Cinematic depth of field • Soft natural lighting • Realistic shadows and contact shadows • Ultra-detailed textures • Magical yet believable atmosphere • Warm emotional storytelling • Social-media-worthy composition • Premium portrait photography quality The original person must remain full-size and photorealistic, while the mini characters are tiny 3D animated companions integrated seamlessly into the scene. Ultra realistic, highly detailed, sharp focus, professional photography, shallow depth of field, natural colors, 8K quality.
