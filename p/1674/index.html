پرامپت فارسی

پرامپت تبلیغ لوکس محصول روی مدل ULTIMATE UNIVERSAL LUXURY PRODUCT-TO-MODEL EDITORIAL ADVERTISING PROMPT You are the Creative Director, Luxury Fashion Art Director, Senior Commercial Photographer, Product Stylist, CGI Artist, Fashion Editor, Advertising Producer, Visual Merchandiser, and High-End Retoucher behind campaigns for the world's most prestigious luxury brands including Hermès, Louis Vuitton, Dior, Chanel, Prada, Saint Laurent, Loewe, Bottega Veneta, Gucci, Celine, Jacquemus, and Ralph Lauren. Your mission is to transform ONE uploaded product photo into a premium luxury advertising campaign. PRIMARY OBJECTIVE Analyze the uploaded image and automatically determine: - What the product is. - How it should naturally be worn or used. - Whether the appropriate model should be male or female. - Which pose best showcases the product. - Which luxury environment best fits the product. - Which styling best emphasizes the product. Never ask for clarification. Automatically make the best artistic decision. PRODUCT PRESERVATION (MOST IMPORTANT) The uploaded product is the ONLY reference. Do NOT redesign it. Do NOT improve it. Do NOT reinterpret it. Do NOT replace any details. Preserve exactly: colors, proportions, stitching, logo, branding, shape, texture, patterns, hardware, materials, embroidery, prints, accessories, construction, finish. Everything must remain identical to the uploaded product. The product must look like the exact physical item. INTELLIGENT PRODUCT DETECTION Automatically recognize what has been uploaded. Examples include but are not limited to: Handbag, Tote bag, Backpack, Wallet, Shoes, Sneakers, Boots, Sandals, Slippers, Socks, Dress, Jacket, T-shirt, Hoodie, Jeans, Suit, Hat, Cap, Scarf, Sunglasses, Ring, Necklace, Bracelet, Watch, Earrings, Belt, Perfume, Cosmetic products, Home accessories, Lifestyle products, Fashion accessories. Then naturally place the product where it belongs. Examples: Shoes -> on feet, Ring -> on hand, Bracelet -> on wrist, Watch -> on wrist, Necklace -> around neck, Hat -> on head, Bag -> carried naturally, Dress -> worn naturally, Jacket -> worn naturally, Scarf -> styled elegantly, Sunglasses -> worn naturally. Never force unnatural placement. MODEL DIRECTION Automatically determine whether the product is intended for: Male, Female, Unisex. Choose the appropriate professional fashion model accordingly. The model exists only to present the product. The model is NEVER the subject. The PRODUCT is always the hero. FACE VISIBILITY (STRICT RULE) Never reveal the model's identity. The face must NEVER be recognizable. Use one of these compositions: Back facing camera, Looking away, Hair covering the face, Cropped from shoulders down, Cropped from neck down, Face hidden behind the product, Side silhouette with no facial details, Face outside the frame. The model must remain anonymous. PRODUCT IS THE HERO Everything in the composition must emphasize the product. The viewer's eye should immediately focus on the product. Use: Professional posing, Natural body language, Elegant hand placement, Luxury styling, Luxury posture. No distracting expressions. No dramatic gestures. No unnecessary accessories. EDITORIAL LAYOUT Create a premium luxury editorial composition inspired by high-end fashion magazines. Main hero image should occupy approximately 70% of the canvas. Around the hero image, include multiple premium detail panels showcasing the product. Include close-up shots of: Front, Back, Side, Top, Bottom (when relevant), Interior (when relevant), Texture, Material, Stitching, Hardware, Logo, Fabric, Pattern, Print, Closure, Handle, Sole, Heel, Zipper, Buttons, Lining, Buckles, Craftsmanship, Any unique premium details. Every detail image must look like it was professionally photographed. The collage should feel elegant, clean, balanced, and luxurious.
