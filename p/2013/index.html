پرامپت فارسی

A white male, late 20s, olive Mediterranean skin with visible natural texture and light stubble shadow, photographed in strict right-side profile. Thick jet-black hair in a wet-look finish - heavily gelled and pushed up and back into a soft wave at the crown, individual damp strands separated and glossy, loose wet curls flicking outward above the ear and at the nape, short clean sideburn. Prominent straight Roman nose, full dark well-groomed mustache, no beard, medium stubble across the jaw and cheek, full lips slightly parted. Expression: chin level, looking straight ahead past the right edge of the frame, mouth relaxed and closed. Composed, self-possessed, giving nothing away. Pose: right hand raised to the ear, thumb and index finger pinching the stem of a white wireless earbud as he seats it into the ear canal, wrist bent, elbow lifted so the forearm rises through the lower left of the frame. Accessories: black acetate sunglasses in a squared Wayfarer shape with thick temple arms, fine white maker's script printed along the arm, dark lenses. A small ribbed gold huggie hoop in the earlobe. Plain polished gold band on the ring finger. Fine dark hand tattoos - serif capital letters on the back of the hand below the thumb, a scatter of small dots across the finger, and a delicate olive branch and leaves running up the inner wrist. On the wrist, a gold toned-cased dress watch - rounded rectangular case, cream dial with black Roman numerals and slim hands, worn loose on a thick black leather strap with a squared keeper. Outfit: soft-shouldered brown tweed blazer with a fine flecked nap and a notched lapel, sleeve ending mid-forearm. Pale blue cotton shirt beneath with a crisp turned cuff. Dark silk tie in deep burgundy-brown ground printed with gold, teal-green and cream medallion crests, knotted with a clean dimple and sitting in the open V of the jacket. Setting: a facade clad in large rectangular travertine limestone panels filling the entire background - warm cream and grey mottled stone with natural pitting, fine horizontal and vertical joint lines running through the frame. A small brushed steel bracket fixed to the stone at the top edge, and a blown-out slice of white daylight in the top left corner. Shot angle: camera held low and close on his right, angled up at the profile so the head sits high in the frame with the raised hand and watch filling the lower half, jacket shoulder and lapel across the bottom edge. Lighting: hard direct sun from the front - brilliant specular highlights along the gelled hair, the bridge of the nose, the cheekbone, the gold watch case and the white earbud, crisp defined shadow under the jaw, along the neck and beneath the raised forearm. Wall behind evenly sunlit and warm. Sharp high-resolution capture, natural skin texture, warm sunlit grade. Photorealistic.
