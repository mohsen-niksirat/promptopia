پرامپت فارسی

Use the uploaded photo as the sole identity reference. Create a POP MART Hirono-style designer vinyl figure of the subject while preserving the exact facial identity, hairstyle, outfit, accessories, and pose. Place the finished Hirono figure sitting naturally beside the real person from the original photo. Keep the original outdoor background exactly as it is. Do not replace the environment. The real person must remain completely photorealistic and unchanged. Only the figure should be stylized as an official Hirono collectible. Match the figure's pose to the real subject, positioned closely beside them with natural spacing and scale (approximately 35–45 cm tall like a real collectible toy). Soft golden-hour lighting consistent with the original photo. Natural shadows on the grass. The figure should appear physically present in the scene. Official POP MART Hirono designer vinyl style: matte porcelain-like vinyl oversized head sleepy half-closed eyes tiny pout sculpted chunky hair pastel hand-painted finish subtle melancholic expression Do not alter the real person's appearance. Do not create two real people. Do not stylize the background. Only one real person and one Hirono vinyl figure.
