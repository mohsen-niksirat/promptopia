پرامپت فارسی

Restore and colorize this historical photograph while preserving 100% of the original composition, camera angle, perspective, architecture, people, vehicles, signs, and every authentic detail. Do not add, remove, replace, or alter any objects. Perform museum-grade photo restoration by removing scratches, dust, stains, tears, film grain, noise, blur, fading, and compression artifacts while maintaining the original texture. Apply historically accurate colorization using realistic skin tones, clothing colors, architectural materials, vegetation, road surfaces, vehicles, and environmental lighting appropriate for the era and location. Avoid oversaturation or artificial colors. Enhance fine details including faces, hair, fabrics, bricks, wood, metal, glass, stone, shadows, reflections, and depth while preserving authenticity. Correct exposure, white balance, dynamic range, contrast, and lighting naturally. The final result should look like a genuine modern high-end DSLR photograph captured at the same moment in history. Ultra realistic, museum-quality restoration, 8K resolution, ultra sharp, photorealistic, cinematic natural lighting, maximum detail, HDR, true-to-life colors, no AI artifacts, no hallucinations, no added objects, no text changes.
