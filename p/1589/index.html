Ultra-realistic editorial portrait of a young woman standing outdoors on a rooftop at sunset, holding a large bouquet of blooming white lilies over one shoulder. She looks directly into the camera with a soft confident smile while gently touching her hair. Cinematic composition, shallow depth of field, soft atmospheric haze, premium fashion editorial style, 85mm lens, f/1.8, Kodak Portra color grading, HDR, ultra-realistic, highly detailed, no AI look.
