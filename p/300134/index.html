پرامپت فارسی

Create a premium high-contrast monochrome vector-art portrait using the uploaded image as the only identity reference. Composition & Identity: The portrait should be a vertical 4:5 composition, a tight shoulder-up shot with centered framing, presenting a premium poster aesthetic with a clean studio feel. The identity from the reference image must be strictly preserved, translating the exact facial structure, face shape, hairstyle, hairline, eyebrows, eyes, nose, lips, jawline, skin tone, age appearance, and recognizable features into monochrome. Do not redesign or beautify the facial features; the final artwork must immediately resemble the reference person. Style & Execution: Render the entire portrait in high-contrast monochrome black and white, executed as a clean vector illustration. Use sharp cel-shading with bold graphic shapes and crisp, clean edges. Smooth gradients should be replaced entirely with flat shadow planes. This should have a modern comic-book aesthetic and a premium editorial vector style. Key Visual Elements: Rim Lighting: Strong, vibrant, glowing red neon rim lighting must outline the hair, ears, jawline, neck, shoulders, and upper body, creating dramatic contrast against the background and providing realistic edge illumination. Wardrobe: The subject should be wearing an open dark button-up shirt over a clean white T-shirt, with a minimalist square pendant chain necklace, all simplified into clean monochrome vector shapes with modern fashion styling. Background: The background must be pure, matte black, completely clean, with no textures, gradients, objects, or distractions. Lighting & Mood: Employ dramatic studio lighting with high contrast and bold shadow separation. Add cinematic edge lighting to create a premium editorial mood. The final result should be an ultra-clean graphic design with ultra-detailed 8K quality, suitable for a gallery-quality poster.
