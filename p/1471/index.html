پرامپت فارسی

Transform the uploaded reference image into a premium, ultra-realistic cinematic travel-memory photograph. Use the uploaded image as the only source of truth. Preserve the exact moment, people, location, objects, camera perspective and overall composition. The result must clearly look like an enhanced cinematic version of the same photograph—not a completely different scene. Scene Lock — Highest Priority Preserve exactly: the same number of people each person’s identity and appearance hairstyles, skin tones and body proportions clothing colours and clothing styles poses, actions and body positions important objects, vehicles and surroundings camera angle and viewing perspective the relationship and distance between every subject the original location and recognisable environmental details Do not replace the people, redesign their clothing, change their poses or move them into a different environment. Cinematic Transformation Upgrade the original photograph with realistic cinematic lighting, natural atmospheric depth and professional colour grading. Create: richer environmental depth realistic directional natural light soft warm highlights deeper but detailed shadows natural atmospheric haze improved sky detail when visible realistic reflections on wet surfaces subtle separation between foreground, subject and background delicate highlight bloom restrained analogue film grain premium travel-documentary photography quality The transformation should feel emotionally stronger while remaining believable. Adapt the lighting naturally to the uploaded environment. Do not automatically add mountains, rain, fog or sunset if they do not logically belong in the original scene. If the image already contains rain, clouds, mist, water or wet surfaces, enhance those existing elements realistically without changing the location. Colour Grade Use a restrained cinematic palette: natural forest greens earthy browns muted blues and greys warm cream highlights realistic skin tones slightly desaturated shadows controlled contrast Avoid excessive orange-and-teal grading, oversaturation, crushed blacks or artificial HDR. Composition final vertical 4:5 aspect ratio visually equivalent to 1080 × 1350 preserve the original camera angle keep every important person and object fully visible maintain natural perspective and scale create a clear focal point keep enough negative space around the subject produce a sharp, full-bleed image extending naturally to every edge If the uploaded photograph is not already 4:5, extend the real environment naturally to create the required composition. Do not stretch the image, crop important subjects, add borders, create blurred side extensions or use mirrored padding. Realism The final image must look like a genuine photograph captured during the same real moment. Maintain: natural skin texture believable human anatomy accurate hands and feet realistic clothing folds authentic water, rain, mist and reflections physically correct shadows realistic environmental textures subtle phone-camera imperfections Avoid CGI rendering, plastic skin, artificial sharpness, fantasy lighting, fake-looking sun rays or overly polished stock-photo aesthetics. Important Generate only the clean cinematic After image. Do not add: a Before image or inset BEFORE or AFTER labels handwritten phrases or arrows Higgsfield branding promotional text logos watermarks borders or graphic elements Avoid Do not alter the original identities, add or remove people, change outfits, replace the location, invent major objects, modify vehicles, change the central action, create impossible lighting, distort anatomy or turn the photograph into digital art. Goal: Create a beautiful and emotionally cinematic version of the uploaded photograph while making it unmistakably the same people, same location, same action and same real moment.
