پرامپت فارسی

Use the attached image ONLY as a style and composition reference. Create a photorealistic smartphone photo of a young woman in a parking garage, captured through the side mirror of a car. The entire scene should feel candid, natural, and effortlessly stylish, like a spontaneous Instagram post rather than a professional photoshoot. SUBJECT & POSE: The girl stands beside her car, visible only through the car's side mirror. One hip slightly shifted to create a relaxed S-curve posture. Both hands raised near her hair, casually fixing or fluffing it. Head tilted slightly downward or to one side. Soft, natural expression rather than an exaggerated smile. Full upper body and partial lower body visible inside the mirror reflection. Relaxed, feminine body language with a confident but effortless vibe. OUTFIT STYLE: Trendy everyday feminine streetwear. Fitted sleeveless or halter-style top. Mid-rise or low-rise denim jeans. Small shoulder handbag hanging naturally from one shoulder. Minimal accessories. Modern Instagram-girl aesthetic. Clothing should feel wearable and realistic rather than fashion-editorial. CAMERA COMPOSITION: Entire photo is shot from inside a parked car. The car side mirror acts as a natural frame around the subject. Part of the car interior remains visible in the foreground. The mirror occupies a large portion of the image while preserving the surrounding environment. Imperfect smartphone framing is encouraged. Slight handheld imperfections are welcome. LIGHTING: Strong warm red lighting illuminating the girl from one side, similar to brake lights, taillights, or neon reflections. Background remains naturally lit with soft daylight entering the parking structure. Mix of cool ambient daylight and warm red practical lighting. Natural shadow falloff. No studio lighting. No cinematic Hollywood lighting. ENVIRONMENT: Concrete parking garage. Open areas with sunlight visible in the distance. Structural beams and pillars overhead. Real-world imperfections. Urban lifestyle aesthetic. Authentic everyday setting. COLOR GRADING: Warm Instagram aesthetic. Slightly muted colors. Natural skin tones. Soft contrast. Gentle highlight roll-off. Real smartphone HDR processing. No excessive saturation. No teal-and-orange cinematic grading. Modern lifestyle photography color palette. CAMERA QUALITY: Mid-range smartphone camera quality. Slight image softness. Natural compression artifacts. Subtle digital noise. Realistic mobile dynamic range. No ultra-sharp DSLR details. No AI hyper-realism. Looks like an actual photo someone casually took on their phone. FINAL STYLE: Ultra-photorealistic, candid Instagram lifestyle photography, authentic smartphone aesthetics, natural imperfections, effortless feminine confidence, modern urban girl aesthetic, not a professional fashion campaign
