پرامپت فارسی

A high-quality, realistic photograph of a stylish young woman sitting on a rock edge on a cliffside on a foggy, overcast day. She is wearing an oversized black hoodie, black leggings, and a black baseball cap. Her long, dark hair is styled out from under the cap. She is looking directly at the camera with a confident, serious expression. She has on clean, bulky white chunky sneakers. The background is a dramatic rocky cliffside obscured by a deep, atmospheric fog. Lighting is soft, natural, and diffuse. Low-angle shot to emphasize her presence. Detailed texture on the rocks and the hoodie fabric. Ultra-photorealism, shallow depth of field.
