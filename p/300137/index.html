پرامپت فارسی

Create a surreal vertical portrait of the person (use the loaded face) in a charcoal sketch style. Keep the main face sharp and detailed in the center, with bold, dark lines and deep shading to emphasize texture and contrast. Add blurred, semi-transparent copies of the face and upper body, fading on either side to suggest movement or identity change, using soft, smudged charcoal strokes. The background should be a subtle gradient of light gray, with minimal detail to enhance the melancholic mood. Dress the person in a dark, modern outfit, rendered with heavy, textured shading. Use dramatic lighting on the face with stark contrasts between light and shadow to create depth. Mood: cinematic, melancholic, and mysterious.
