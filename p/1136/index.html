پرامپت فارسی

The person's actual physical characteristics must control the final feline counterpart. Different person = different cat. The cat must be recognizable as the feline version of this specific person, not as a generic cat and not as a predetermined cat template. IMPORTANT: Use ONLY the person's natural physical appearance and facial characteristics as inspiration for the cat's appearance. Analyze the person's actual face, eye color, eye shape and proportions, facial proportions, skin tone as a reference for fur coloration, natural hair color as a reference for fur color and pattern, and overall body proportions to create a unique feline counterpart. CLOTHING AND ACCESSORIES MUST NOT BE TRANSFERRED TO THE CAT: Do NOT put any clothing, scarf, headscarf, hijab, shawl, hat, coat, shirt, dress, pants, shoes, jewelry, glasses, hair accessories, or any other human accessory on the cat. The cat must be completely naturally furry and fully feline, with no human clothing or accessories whatsoever. If the person is wearing a scarf, headscarf, hijab, or any other garment in the reference image, completely ignore it when designing the cat's appearance. Do not copy the garment, its color, texture, shape, or pattern onto the cat. The cat must have natural fur covering its body instead. The person's natural hair color may influence the cat's fur color and natural fur pattern, but the hairstyle itself must NOT be copied. The person's facial features should influence the feline equivalent of the cat's eyes, eye color, eye proportions, facial structure, muzzle shape, and overall appearance while remaining completely anatomically feline. The final result must be a real domestic cat with authentic feline eyes, natural feline pupils, a real feline skull and facial structure, natural cat ears, a real feline nose and muzzle, realistic whiskers, anatomically correct feline body proportions, and realistic fur covering the entire body. ENVIRONMENT PRESERVATION: Keep the exact original location and environment from the reference image. Preserve the same room or location, background, furniture and objects, architecture, composition, camera angle, camera perspective, lighting direction and quality, shadows, color atmosphere, and depth of field. Do not replace, redesign, or reinterpret the environment. Only transform the person into their natural feline counterpart. The rest of the image should remain as close as possible to the original reference. The cat should occupy the same general position and scale as the person and naturally fit into the original environment. Preserve the person's pose and body orientation as closely as naturally possible for a real cat, but adapt the anatomy realistically to a feline body. Ultra-photorealistic, lifelike real domestic cat, natural animal anatomy, realistic fur texture, detailed whiskers, physically accurate lighting, natural photographic detail, DSLR-quality photography, realistic depth of field, premium animal portrait photography, highly detailed, photorealistic.
