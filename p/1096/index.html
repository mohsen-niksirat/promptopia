پرامپت فارسی

Create an ultra-realistic, DSLR-quality, black-and-white studio wedding portrait in 9:16 vertical format. Use ONLY the uploaded face photo(s) as the identity reference. Replace the bride and groom’s faces with the uploaded face(s) while preserving their exact facial structure, eye shape, skin texture, nose, lips, eyebrows, hairline, and overall identity. Do not alter or beautify their facial features beyond natural professional photography. Maintain realistic skin pores, fine facial hair, natural imperfections, and authentic facial proportions. Composition: Upper-body portrait (from about the waist/stomach up). Couple standing close together in a romantic pose. Both facing directly toward the camera. Soft, genuine, elegant smiles. Bride slightly leaning toward the groom. Groom standing confidently beside her. Hands & Rings: Both extend one hand toward the camera with a relaxed closed fist (not tightly clenched), creating a professional wedding pose. Their wedding rings are clearly visible on the ring fingers. Bride wears a delicate feminine wedding band. Groom wears a simple masculine wedding band. The hands appear naturally closer to the camera with realistic perspective and shallow depth of field. Bride: Beautiful elegant bridal makeup with a soft natural (light/glam) look. Professional bridal hairstyle: luxurious voluminous low updo (chignon) with soft face-framing strands. Hair color: brown with blonde highlights (balayage). Small minimalist crystal bridal tiara. Long veil. Modest luxury wedding dress with high neckline and long sleeves. Elegant floral lace embroidered tulle covering the sleeves and upper chest. No revealing neckline. Holding a minimalist bouquet of fresh white roses. Groom: Classic luxury black tuxedo. White dress shirt. Black bow tie. Elegant white rose boutonniere with greenery on the left lapel. Clean, premium tailoring. Lighting & Style: Professional studio portrait with extremely realistic lighting. Soft diffused key light. Gentle contrast. Natural skin tones before monochrome conversion. Premium full-frame DSLR camera look. 85mm portrait lens. f/1.8 shallow depth of field. Razor-sharp focus on faces and rings. Soft background blur. Background: Outdoor garden-inspired background with softly blurred trees and foliage. Minimal distractions. Elegant wedding atmosphere. Color: High-end monochrome (black & white). Rich tonal range. Deep blacks. Soft whites. Fine grayscale transitions. Timeless luxury wedding photography aesthetic. Quality: Hyper-realistic, photorealistic, authentic wedding photography, premium studio quality, magazine-level detail, natural posing, cinematic realism, highly detailed skin texture, realistic hands and fingers, anatomically correct proportions, realistic wedding rings, no AI artifacts, no extra fingers, no distorted hands, no plastic skin, no cartoon effect, true-to-life DSLR photograph.
