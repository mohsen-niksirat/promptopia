پرامپت فارسی

Extreme low-angle selfie shot pointing upward toward sky. Vivid clear bright blue sky fills entire background. Two palm trees visible at upper corners, green fronds extending inward. Very sunny, midday tropical light. Subject: young male, mid-20s, medium-tan olive skin, short dark hair, full beard and neat mustache. Face fills lower two-thirds of frame. Hat: wide-brim bucket hat in cream/off-white canvas with raw frayed unfinished edges all around brim, intentionally distressed. Small embossed text on front band reading "JACQUEMUS" in block letters. Thick cream cotton cord chin strap hanging loosely at neck. Sunglasses: sporty wrap-style with pale yellow/cream frames and mirrored amber/gold lenses, reflective orange-gold tint. Slightly futuristic silhouette. Jewelry: chunky shell and bead necklace in white, yellow, and brown tones. Silver chain with small rectangular pendant or lock charm. Small pearl or white stud earring visible. Top: cream/off-white loose knit mesh or crochet tank top, open weave texture, relaxed fit. Brown leather crossbody bag strap diagonally across chest. Expression: lips very slightly parted, neutral to soft. Direct gaze into camera. Face fully in sun, warm light on skin. Harsh bright tropical sunlight, strong natural highlights, slight overexposure on hat and skin. Vivid saturated colors. Slightly editorial lifestyle photography. Photorealistic.
