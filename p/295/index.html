مدل مو بارانیMan Portrait Summer

پرامپتوپیا · گالری پرامپت‌های آماده هوش مصنوعی

پرامپت فارسی

Use the reference face exactly. Preserve facial identity, facial proportions, hairstyle, skin tone, age, and every recognizable feature. Create an ultra-realistic luxury editorial magazine cover inspired by Japanese summer films. The viewer stands outside a rain-free window, looking through the glass at a handsome young man sitting close to the windowsill. Close-up portrait (face fills 65–70% of the frame), slightly high angle. He gently rests his chin on folded arms, first gazing peacefully outside before naturally meeting the camera with a genuine, heartwarming smile, as if welcoming someone he has been waiting for. His expressive eyes are the main focus. Soft golden summer afternoon sunlight wraps around his face with realistic natural shadows. The glass shows delicate reflections of lush green trees, blue sky, and tiny light sparkles without hiding his face. Warm ivory, cream, olive-green, and pastel tones, authentic skin texture, subtle cinematic film grain, soft contrast, creamy shallow depth of field, quiet nostalgic atmosphere, premium lifestyle photography. Clean minimalist luxury magazine cover with elegant serif title "The Moment" at the top-left and a refined subtitle beneath, generous negative space, award-winning composition, photorealistic, cinematic, 8K quality, 3:4 aspect ratio. Avoid AI-looking skin, altered facial features, exaggerated eyes, HDR, oversaturation, heavy bokeh, strong lens flare, cluttered backgrounds, or reflections covering the face.
