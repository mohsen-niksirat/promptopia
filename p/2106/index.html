A young woman with a youthful feminine appearance. The image is a delicate, classic close-up selfie taken from a straight-on front angle in a cozy indoor setting, highlighting a soft, feminine look that blends simplicity with warm elegance. She is seated in a relaxed position, leaning against a soft pillow, with her head slightly tilted, looking directly into the camera with a soft, neutral expression. She wears a simple, fitted black camisole with thin straps and a wide round neckline that elegantly highlights her neck and shoulders. She accessorizes with a very delicate gold chain featuring an open-heart pendant resting gently at the center of her chest and large gold hoop earrings visible between strands of her hair, adding a timeless feminine touch. Her dark hair is casually tied back in an effortless updo, with long, softly wavy face-framing strands surrounding her face and forehead. Her skin is fresh and dewy with warm peachy-pink blush creating a natural glow, thick lifted eyelashes, thin eyeliner, naturally shaped eyebrows, and soft matte nude-pink lips. She is seated in a warm indoor environment with a sheer white curtain featuring soft vertical folds directly behind her, while part of a heavy emerald-green velvet curtain appears on the right side in the background, creating a rich, warm color contrast. Soft, diffused indoor lighting gently highlights her facial features and flawless skin while creating a subtle glow on her gold jewelry without harsh or distracting shadows. The image has a soft, slightly hazy texture that mimics analog film cameras or a subtle vintage filter. Aspect ratio 9:16, natural skin texture, no artificial plastic look.
