پرامپت فارسی

Create a custom Instagram sticker pack based on my Instagram page identity and the sticker topic I provide. Instagram ID: [آیدی اینستاگرام] Sticker theme/topic: [موضوع یا سوال استیکر] No reference image is needed. Create the entire sticker design from scratch based on the Instagram page identity, target audience, and the provided sticker topic. Use this aesthetic style: cute kawaii sticker style soft pastel colors clean white sticker border rounded organic shapes minimal but attractive design small cute characters with expressive faces smooth vector illustration style cozy Instagram-friendly design premium handmade kawaii feeling Design stickers that perfectly match my page theme and audience. Include creative visual elements related to the topic, cute characters, relevant icons, and short readable text phrases if needed. Requirements: Make the stickers look like professional Instagram story stickers. Keep them simple, recognizable, and eye-catching at small size. Use transparent background outside the sticker shape. Create a consistent visual identity across all stickers with matching colors, fonts, and character style. Make the designs unique and original; do not copy existing stickers. Ensure the stickers are optimized for Instagram Stories and easy to read on mobile screens. Create [بنویس چه تعداد] different sticker designs suitable for Instagram stories.
