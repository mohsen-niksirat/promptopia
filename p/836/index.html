عکس استودیوییPalm Photo Studio

پرامپتوپیا · گالری پرامپت‌های آماده هوش مصنوعی

پرامپت فارسی

Transform the uploaded person into a beautiful handcrafted claymation character while preserving 100% facial identity from the reference photo. IDENTITY PRESERVATION: Use the uploaded photo as the main facial identity reference. Preserve the exact face shape, eyes, nose, mouth, eyebrows, hairline, hairstyle, and overall recognizable expression structure. The person must remain instantly identifiable as the same real person, translated into a stylized clay stop-motion character. STYLE: Authentic premium claymation / stop-motion look. The character should look hand-sculpted from modeling clay, with smooth clay surfaces, subtle handmade imperfections, soft rounded forms, sculpted hair, clay eyelids, clay lips, and carefully shaped facial features. Keep it cute, polished, charming, and visually appealing. Not flat 2D, not realistic skin, not painterly, not plastic toy-like, not futuristic sci-fi, not outer space themed. It should feel like a beautifully photographed stop-motion clay figure. POSE: Generate only ONE pose. Randomly choose one expressive and natural pose each time, such as standing casually, smiling, leaning, walking, sitting, looking around, holding hands in pockets, laughing, or reacting naturally. Each generation should use a different random pose. OUTFIT: Use everyday stylish clothing or casual fashionable clothes. Keep the outfit simple, attractive, and made from colored clay. No sci-fi outfit, no futuristic suit, no armor, no space gear. ENVIRONMENT: Generate only ONE background. Randomly choose one realistic, grounded, everyday, or cozy setting each time, such as a street, park, cafe, home interior, studio, bookstore, old town alley, garden, beach, autumn walkway, or cozy room. The whole environment should also feel handcrafted from clay miniatures. No outer space, no alien planet, no portal room, no futuristic hallway, no sci-fi lab. LIGHTING: Soft cinematic lighting suitable for clay stop-motion. Gentle highlights on clay surfaces, subtle shadows, warm dimensionality, natural and cozy mood. COMPOSITION: Vertical 4:5 composition. Single character focus. Clean readable silhouette. The final result should look like a high-quality still frame from a beautiful stop-motion clay animation film. IMPORTANT: No text anywhere in the image. No captions, no signs, no logos, no watermark, no speech bubbles. RANDOMIZATION: Keep the same person’s identity consistent, but randomize the pose and background on every generation within realistic non-sci-fi everyday settings only.
