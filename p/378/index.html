مدل مو برفیWoman Portrait Street

پرامپت فارسی

Use the uploaded person image as the ONLY strict identity reference. Preserve the exact facial features, face shape, eyes, eyebrows, nose, lips, skin texture, age, hair color, hairstyle and overall identity with maximum resemblance. The woman must be instantly recognizable as the reference image. Do not beautify, retouch or alter facial proportions in any way. Maintain the exact appearance of the uploaded reference person. Ultra-realistic cinematic urban fashion portrait of the reference woman standing alone at a busy city crosswalk during daytime. The woman wears an elegant oversized ivory wool coat layered over a minimalist cream outfit. Round dark sunglasses and subtle red lipstick add a timeless editorial fashion aesthetic. She stands still among a constantly moving crowd of pedestrians dressed in dark winter clothing. The camera is positioned high above the street, looking downward from an elevated perspective. The woman looks directly upward toward the camera, immediately becoming the visual focal point of the scene. Hundreds of pedestrians move around her. The surrounding crowd is rendered with realistic motion blur and soft focus, creating the feeling that the entire city is moving while she remains perfectly still. Strong zebra crossing lines create bold graphic patterns beneath her feet. The woman remains sharp while the people closest to the camera become heavily blurred foreground shapes, creating cinematic depth and separation. Atmosphere: urban isolation, confidence, individuality, fashion-forward elegance, modern city life, the feeling of standing apart from the crowd. Natural daylight mixed with soft city reflections. Slight atmospheric haze softens the distance. Color palette is critically important: bright ivory coat, deep charcoal clothing on surrounding pedestrians, cool gray asphalt, soft muted city tones, subtle warm skin tones, minimal but elegant color accents. The white/ivory coat should visually separate the woman from the dark crowd, creating immediate contrast and visual hierarchy. Inspired by Vogue street editorials, Fujifilm GFX campaigns, Peter Lindbergh, modern luxury fashion photography and cinematic urban storytelling. Shot from an overhead perspective with a medium telephoto lens. Strong compression, layered depth, selective focus, realistic crowd movement. Fujifilm GFX 100 aesthetic, ultra-detailed skin texture, photorealistic lighting, realistic motion blur, editorial fashion photography, cinematic masterpiece, award-winning street portrait, vertical composition 9:16. Additional Banana Pro emphasis: HIGH-ANGLE OVERHEAD VIEW, WOMAN LOOKING UP AT CAMERA, SHARP SUBJECT SURROUNDED BY MOTION-BLURRED CROWD, IVORY COAT CONTRAST AGAINST DARK PEDESTRIANS, CINEMATIC STREET PHOTOGRAPHY, URBAN ISOLATION, VOGUE EDITORIAL AESTHETIC, FUJIFILM GFX LOOK, GRAPHIC CROSSWALK PATTERNS, DEPTH THROUGH FOREGROUND BLUR, FEMININE ELEGANCE. Negative prompt: text, watermark, logo, extra people in focus, duplicated faces, distorted anatomy, beauty filter, CGI appearance, cartoon, illustration, low quality, altered identity, oversaturated colors, unrealistic crowd behavior, masculine features.
