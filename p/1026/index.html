پرامپت فارسی

Upload one photo of yourself, or a photo of you and your partner. Create an ultra-realistic prediction of your future child at about 3 years old. Generate TWO versions: a boy and a girl. Keep skin texture, facial details, lighting, and anatomy highly realistic with natural photography quality. Add elegant Persian (Farsi) typography and labels. Create a premium family-portrait style layout showing: * Future Son (3 years old) * Future Daughter (3 years old) If only one person is uploaded, analyze which facial traits (eyes, nose, lips, face shape, hair, smile, etc.) the children likely inherited from that person and explain in Persian. If two people are uploaded, analyze both faces and explain in Persian which features of each child likely came from each parent. Present the explanation as a clean Persian infographic with beautiful typography and realistic visual design.
