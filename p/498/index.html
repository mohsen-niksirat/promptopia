پرامپت فارسی

Edit the uploaded image for an Instagram Stories-style post by adding chalk-like handwritten text and simple doodles. Keep the original image completely unchanged (subject, face, pose, outfit, lighting, background, composition). Only add decorative overlays. Add 2–5 short, context-aware captions based on what is visible in the photo (mood, expression, outfit, setting). Make the text personal, cute, funny, and natural—not generic. Style: minimal Gen Z chalk aesthetic with soft white/pastel tones (white, cream, pink, baby blue, light yellow) and a slightly grainy chalk texture. Add small hearts, stars, sparkles, arrows, circles, underlines, and wavy lines. Placement: put text and doodles in empty spaces around the subject. Do not cover the face or important details. Keep it balanced and readable, not crowded. Format: vertical 4:5 Instagram composition within safe margins. Important: no new objects or redesigns—only subtle chalk-style annotations that feel naturally integrated.
