پرامپت فارسی

Ultra-realistic modern wedding photography portrait, built 100% from the unaltered facial features of the reference image(s); use the uploaded reference as the ONLY identity source. Preserve 100% facial identity and natural facial structure for both subjects (no changes to face shape, lips, eyes, eyebrows, nose, skin texture) and keep hair/hairstyle and beard (if any) exactly as the reference (absolutely no hair/beard changes). Scene: loving couple sitting side-by-side inside a classic cream-colored car with brown leather seats; romantic decorated wedding-car details (subtle ribbons/flowers, no readable text). Bride: wearing a white guipure lace wedding dress with a long veil, holding a bouquet of pink-and-white centaurea-style flowers. Groom: wearing a cream suit with a white shirt and a cream bow tie. Setting outside the windows: open street-like space with many fallen leaves on the ground, warm nostalgic autumn vibe. Camera: half-up view from the driver’s-side angle, focusing on faces, clothing textures, and intimacy. Lighting/style: natural soft daylight with gentle contrast, warm cinematic color grading, elegant modern wedding look, shallow depth of field, fine detail in lace, veil, leather seats, and bouquet. Output: ultra-high-definition photorealism (8K-level detail), portrait aspect ratio 2:3. Negative prompt: any face/identity change, face reshaping, lip change, hair/beard change, skin smoothing/retouching, beauty filters, de-aging, face-swap artifacts, CGI/cartoon, HDR glow, oversharpening, blur on faces, noise artifacts, distortion, incorrect anatomy, extra fingers/limbs, logos, readable text, watermark, branding.
