مرجع چهره آپلود شده بدون هیچ تغییری باشه وجزییات مو و صورت حفظ شه A highly detailed, dramatic, cinematic close-up portrait of a young woman with a strong, intense gaze. ​Subject Details: ​Facial Expression: A captivating, serious, and slightly sultry expression. The gaze is direct and piercing. ​Lips: Full lips with a deep, matte, reddish-brown/bordeaux lipstick color, slightly parted. ​Hair: Dark, wind-blown hair, creating a sense of movement. Strands are intentionally covering part of her face, especially one eye and across the nose/mouth area, adding mystery and a dynamic texture. ​Skin Texture: Flawless, luminous, and smooth skin, with clear, defined highlights and shadows, emphasizing the bone structure (especially the cheekbones). ​Lighting & Mood: ​Lighting Style: Chiaroscuro and Split Lighting (or Paramount/Butterfly lighting with a deep falloff). A single, strong, softbox/beauty dish light source is positioned to the side and slightly above, creating deep, dramatic, and well-defined shadows (shadows on the opposite side are almost pure black). ​Background: Solid, deep, dark, and featureless black (or very dark charcoal gray) to allow the subject to stand out completely. Low-key style. ​Color Palette: Warm, earthy, and rich tones. Dominant colors are deep browns, warm golden/amber skin tones, and deep red/bordeaux lips against a pitch-black background. ​Technical & Artistic Style: ​Camera: Shot on a high-end Canon EOS R5 or Sony a7R V with a 85mm f/1.4 or 135mm f/1.8 lens. ​Aperture: Extremely shallow depth of field (low f-stop, e.g., f/1.8), ensuring the eyes and lips are razor-sharp while the background is completely blurred (bokeh is non-existent due to the black background). ​Style: Editorial portraiture, Fashion photography, Fine Art Photography, Studio photography. ​Quality: Ultra-realistic, 8k resolution, photorealistic, high contrast, professional retouching.
