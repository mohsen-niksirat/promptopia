پرامپت فارسی

Generate a professional biometric 3×4 identification portrait from the uploaded reference image. Keep the person’s identity and distinctive facial features fully recognizable while correcting the pose into a centered, level, straight-on head-and-shoulders view. The face should appear naturally balanced, both eyes aligned, and the subject must look directly into the camera. Official styling: - Male subject: plain dark formal suit jacket with a clean white collared shirt. - Female subject: simple black maghnaeh or official hijab that fully covers the hair and neck. Expression and visibility: Use a neutral expression with the mouth closed, no smile, and both eyes fully open and unobstructed. Studio setup: Use soft, flat, evenly distributed high-key lighting with no visible shadows. Place the subject against a completely solid pure-white background. Corrections: Level any head tilt, correct camera and perspective distortion, remove eyeglasses, center the face, and tidy temporary skin imperfections while preserving natural texture, age, and identity. Final output: Hyper-realistic official photo, sharp focus, natural unfiltered appearance, high-resolution 8K detail, portrait 3:4 aspect ratio, no text, no watermark, and no decorative elements.
