پرامپت فارسی

Create an ultra-realistic close-up wedding portrait of a bride and groom with a creative layered composition. Place the groom extremely close to the camera in the left foreground, occupying approximately half of the frame. Show only part of his face and upper torso, with the left side of his face cropped outside the image. He has dark neatly styled hair, a full well-groomed black beard, and wears a sophisticated dark navy tuxedo jacket with a subtle dotted texture, a crisp white shirt, and a black bow tie. Position the bride directly behind the groom so that she playfully peeks out from behind his right shoulder. Most of her face must remain concealed by the groom, with only one eye, one eyebrow, part of her nose, cheek, and lips visible. Her visible eye looks directly into the camera with an elegant, confident expression. The bride has warm natural skin, refined bridal makeup, defined eyebrows, long eyelashes, subtle bronze eyeshadow, soft contouring, and nude lipstick. Her dark brown hair is styled in an elegant bridal updo with loose curled strands framing her face. Place a sparkling silver crystal tiara on her head and attach a delicate white bridal veil behind it. Add a long elegant crystal earring to her visible ear. Use a very tight vertical crop from the groom’s chest to slightly above the bride’s tiara. Create strong visual depth through overlapping faces and shoulders. Keep the bride’s visible eye and facial details sharply focused while the groom in the extreme foreground is slightly softer but still recognizable. Use soft diffused indoor wedding lighting, warm neutral color grading, creamy highlights, natural skin texture, subtle background blur, shallow depth of field, luxurious romantic atmosphere, high-end wedding editorial photography, cinematic composition, realistic textile detail, professional skin retouching without plastic texture, DSLR quality, 85mm lens, wide aperture, ultra-detailed, photorealistic, 8K. Vertical portrait, aspect ratio 4:5, no text, no logo, no watermark.
