پرامپت فارسی

Ultra realistic luxury men's fashion editorial collage featuring the same handsome young man in six different cinematic lifestyle portraits, arranged in a clean magazine inspired grid with thin white borders separating each frame. Every panel features the same person, wearing the same outfit, photographed during warm golden hour inside a modern minimalist apartment. The subject has naturally wavy medium length dark brown hair with soft volume and textured curls falling over the forehead, thick eyebrows, warm olive skin, a sharp defined jawline, high cheekbones, straight nose, subtle stubble, full lips, and a calm, mysterious expression. His appearance is effortlessly elegant and masculine. He wears an oversized ivory white linen button up shirt with the top three buttons casually undone, exposing part of the collarbone and chest, sleeves loosely rolled to the forearms, paired with tailored black trousers. Accessories include a stainless steel luxury wristwatch, a thin silver bracelet, a minimalist necklace, and a subtle silver ring. The lighting is entirely natural golden hour sunlight streaming through large windows fitted with Venetian blinds, creating dramatic horizontal shadow patterns across the face, shirt, walls, and furniture. The warm amber sunlight produces cinematic contrast, soft highlights, realistic skin glow, and rich atmospheric depth. Panel 1: Close side profile portrait looking toward the window, strong jawline highlighted by warm sunlight, relaxed expression, dramatic blind shadows crossing the neck and cheek. Panel 2: Medium portrait sitting beside a window, body turned slightly sideways while gazing outside thoughtfully, relaxed posture with one arm resting on the knee. Panel 3: Tight emotional close up with both hands gently covering part of the mouth, luxury wristwatch prominently visible, eyes partially hidden by soft hair, cinematic shadow patterns across the face. Panel 4: Casual mirror selfie holding a black smartphone in one hand while seated indoors, soft sunlight illuminating the shirt and face, relaxed fashion aesthetic. Panel 5: Wide lifestyle portrait sitting comfortably in a vintage wooden chair, legs crossed naturally, one hand touching the face while surrounded by warm evening light, minimalist interior visible. Panel 6: Close portrait from behind and slightly to the side, hand resting on the back of the neck while looking away toward the sunlight, emphasizing profile, neck, and soft hair texture. The environment features warm beige walls, wooden furniture, linen curtains, indoor plants, subtle decor, and a calm minimalist apartment with Scandinavian and Japanese inspired interior design. Soft shadows, realistic room depth, and clean architectural lines create an intimate atmosphere.
