پرامپت فارسی

پرامپت اینسایداوت🏖🪄 Use my uploaded photo as the visual reference for the main character. Analyze my personality based on the information I provide about myself, including my behaviors, fears, motivations, strengths, emotional patterns, habits, and reactions. Imagine that inside my mind there is a magical futuristic "Emotion Control Room". Create a cinematic scene where a character based on me is standing inside this emotional universe. Transform me into an original cute 3D animated character while keeping my recognizable appearance from the photo. Inside this world, create several unique emotion characters inspired by human emotions from Inside Out (such as Joy, Sadness, Anger, Fear, Disgust, Anxiety, Envy and other emotions). Do not copy any existing movie characters. Create completely original designs with different appearances, outfits, personalities and expressions. Analyze my personality and decide which emotion is currently controlling my mind's main console. Show this emotion as the main character sitting at the central control panel. Create a futuristic emotional dashboard around the scene using beautiful floating liquid glass panels. The texts inside the liquid glass panels must be written in Persian language and should feel like a fun personality test result, not a serious psychological report. Include short playful information such as: · 🎮 فرمانده ذهن من: [نام احساس] · 📊 میزان کنترل امروز: [درصد] · 💬 جمله معروف این احساس: [a funny relatable sentence] · ✨ قدرت مخفی: [positive trait] · 🤯 عادت عجیب ذهن: [funny habit] · 🔋 وضعیت احساسی امروز: [creative status] · 🧠 چند درصد مغزم درگیر این احساس است Add smaller emotion characters around the room showing other parts of my personality. Each one should have a unique role in my emotional system. Visual style: High-quality 3D animated movie style, expressive characters, cinematic lighting, futuristic magical environment, emotional storytelling, premium Instagram aesthetic, detailed textures, soft colors, original character design. Composition: Vertical 3:4 Instagram post format. Main character in the center of the Emotion Control Room. The dominant emotion controlling the main console. Other emotions around the room. Floating Persian liquid glass UI panels on the sides. Beautiful depth of field, professional social media design. Make all visible text in the image Persian with readable typography. Do not use English text.
