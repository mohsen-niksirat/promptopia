پرامپت فارسی

I will provide my brand logo, my profession/business description, and optionally a photo of myself. Use them as the primary visual references. Create a premium Pinterest-style animated sticker pack consisting of 12 unique stickers, all arranged together on one clean 16:9 sticker sheet. Identity Preservation (Highest Priority): If a logo is provided, preserve its exact colors, proportions, typography, symbols, and brand identity. If a photo of me is provided, preserve my facial identity accurately. Do not change my face, hairstyle, facial features, skin tone, or recognizable characteristics. Create a cute stylized/cartoon version while keeping me instantly recognizable. Do not invent or replace branding elements. Sticker Style: Modern Pinterest aesthetic. Cute, premium, expressive. High-end animated/cartoon illustration style. Smooth clean outlines. Bright vibrant colors. Soft gradients. Glossy highlights. Soft shadows. Slight 3D depth. High visual appeal. Viral social-media sticker style. Transparent sticker borders with thick white die-cut outline. Each sticker should look like a premium messaging sticker. Content: Design every sticker around my profession and brand. Each sticker should communicate a different emotion, action, or message related to my business. Include a mix of: Happy Excited Working Thinking Celebrating Success Customer service Approval Creative idea Fast delivery / Quick service (if applicable) Love/Thank you Brand mascot pose Make every sticker unique with different poses, facial expressions, gestures, props, and compositions. If my business has specific tools or products, naturally integrate them into the stickers. Composition: Exactly 12 stickers. Equal visual importance. Even spacing. No cropping. No overlap. One single horizontal 16:9 sheet. Clean white background or transparent-looking presentation. Each sticker individually separated. Quality: Ultra high resolution. Professional vector-like quality. Crisp edges. Extremely detailed. Commercial branding quality. Pinterest trending aesthetic. Consistent art style across all 12 stickers. The final output should look like a premium downloadable sticker pack ready for Telegram, WhatsApp, Instagram, iMessage, or Pinterest.
