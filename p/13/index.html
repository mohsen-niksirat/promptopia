پرامپت فارسی

Create an ultra-realistic, high-resolution humorous portrait of a famous football-style athlete with an exaggerated oversized head and a small, realistic body, sitting exhausted on the floor and leaning against a plain light-gray wall. The subject has a tired, lifeless expression with half-closed eyes, slightly open mouth, messy yet stylish short dark hair, subtle facial stubble, and realistic skin texture with visible pores and natural imperfections. He wears a light gray hoodie, matching gray shorts, and white ankle socks. His legs are stretched out casually, arms resting loosely on his thighs, conveying complete exhaustion and zero energy. On the wall beside him is a standard electrical outlet with a white charging cable plugged into it, humorously implying that he needs to recharge like a phone. Above his head floats a clean smartphone-style 1% battery icon with a nearly empty battery outlined in white and a tiny red charge bar, reinforcing the "out of battery" concept. The setting is a modern indoor room with soft beige walls, gray carpet, a large window with natural daylight entering from the left, and neutral-colored curtains. Lighting is soft, cinematic, and natural, creating gentle shadows and realistic depth.
