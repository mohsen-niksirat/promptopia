پرامپت فارسی

EDIT THE PROVIDED IMAGE DO NOT CHANGE THE ORIGINAL SUBJECT, COMPOSITION OR PHOTOGRAPHY. Transform the image by adding tiny, colorful 2000s-style gemstone, rhinestone and acrylic sticker embellishments directly onto the visible subject. The result should feel like an aesthetic Y2K candid photo / cool girl scrapbook aesthetic - playful, slightly chaotic, colourful and effortlessly cute, rather than polished, symmetrical or overly designed. STICKER STYLE Use a mix of 3D-looking flat-back acrylic gems, rhinestones and tiny plastic charms with a glossy, translucent, candy-like finish. They should look like real physical stickers gems photographed on the subject, with subtle highlights, reflections and dimensionality. Mix these shapes: 5-point stars hearts tiny 5-petal flowers tiny moons diamonds / geometric gems tiny irregular crystal shapes round rhinestones rectangular, clouds, and faceted gems Use different sizes, predominantly tiny and small, with a handful of medium statement gems. Avoid making every sticker the same size or shape or colour. Use a bright, candy-coloured Y2K palette, including: baby pink, hot enk cer red, ran, lemon yellow, aqua blue, lavender, clear crystal/ silver. Colours should be mixed randomly throughout. Include transparent and semitransparent gems. FINISH & MATERIAL: The gems should look: glossy, translucent, faceted, slightly reflective, jelly-like / acrylic, candy-coloured, dimensional. HOW TO SCATTER THEM: DO NOT create an evenly spaced pattern. It should be random, scattered, yet covering all part of the subject of the image. Mostly individual stickers, some areas relatively empty, some areas noticeably more decorated, varied spacing, random orientation. The placement should feel hand-applied and organically random, as though someone spent 10 minutes decorating the subject with a packet of cute craft gems. Do not make it symmetrical. Do not create a repetitive grid.
