پرامپت فارسی

A cute minimalist portrait of a young woman sitting casually on the floor in a clean studio setting with a light grey background. She wears an oversized dark charcoal hoodie, cream straight-leg pants, white socks, and chunky white sneakers. Relaxed seated pose with crossed legs, one eye slightly closed in a playful wink and a soft smile. Behind her, a large black-and-white doodle character hugs her from behind, wrapping its cartoon arms around her shoulders and gently squeezing her cheek.
