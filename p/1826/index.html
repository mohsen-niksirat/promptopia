Ultra-realistic high-fashion jewelry editorial portrait of an elegant woman as a reference, photographed in an extreme close-up from the shoulders upward against a clean warm off-white studio background. Preserve the exact composition, pose, low camera angle, tight asymmetric framing, head position, lighting, and sophisticated editorial mood of the reference image. The woman is shown in a dramatic three-quarter side profile with her head tilted slightly backward and turned to the side, elongating her neck and emphasizing the sculptural jawline. Her eyes are softly closed. Her dark hair is sleek, glossy, perfectly smooth, center-parted and pulled tightly back into a low ponytail. A long layer of sheer translucent black tulle drapes loosely over her head and face, falling vertically across the foreground. The delicate mesh remains clearly visible, partially veiling her facial features while allowing the skin, profile, eye, lips, and jewelry to remain visible through the transparent fabric. The tulle creates subtle graphic lines and soft shadows across her face and neck. She wears an extravagant haute joaillerie statement necklace composed of multiple rows of brilliant-cut diamonds with an intricate sculptural centerpiece, paired with dramatic diamond earrings featuring a deep sapphire-blue teardrop gemstone. Thin black dress straps are barely visible at the edges of the frame. Maximum skin realism: authentic visible pores, natural epidermal microtexture, fine peach fuzz, subtle skin irregularities, delicate expression lines, realistic tonal variation, natural lips, physically accurate skin texture, no airbrushing, no excessive smoothing, no waxy or plastic skin. Hard directional studio light creates strong sculptural highlights and deep controlled shadows, emphasizing the contours of her face, neck, gemstones, and transparent tulle. Slight vintage photographic grain, subtle analog texture, luxury 1990s fashion magazine aesthetic, high-end jewelry campaign, Vogue-style editorial photography, realistic diamond refractions and reflections, crisp jewelry details, cinematic contrast, photorealistic, ultra-high resolution, no text, no watermark.
