پرامپت فارسی

Ultra-cinematic close-up portrait of a young man, face filling the frame, with slightly messy wavy hair falling across the forehead. His expression is calm, introspective, and mysterious. A dramatic beam of warm golden-orange light cuts horizontally across his eyes, illuminating only the eye area while the rest of the face is enveloped in deep teal-green shadow The contrast between warm and cool tones creates a strong cinematic, moody atmosphere. Skin texture is highly detailed and realistic, with visible pores and subtle imperfections The lighting is soft but directional, creating smooth gradients across the face. Background is dark, minimal, and out of focus emphasizing the glowing eyes and emotional depth. Color palette: teal shadows + amber/orange eye light. Mood: mysterious, cinematic, futuristic, introspective. Camera & Image Settings Use these settings (or similar equivalents): Camera Lens: 85mm portrait lens Aperture: f/1.8 - f/2.2 ISO: 100-200 Shutter: 1/125s Focus: eyes Lighting Main light: narrow warm orange beam across the eyes Secondary fil: soft teal ambient light from side or below High contrast ratio (4:1 or stronger) Color grading Teal shadows Warm amber highlights Slight vignette High contrast, cinematic tone curve
