عکستو میدی به استایلت نمره میدهI will upload a photo of myself

پرامپت فارسی

I will upload a photo of myself.YOUR TASK IS TO CREATE AN ACTUAL IMAGE.Do NOT give me a written analysis.Do NOT answer with text.Do NOT describe the collage. Do NOT give me the analysis separately. GENERATE ONE SINGLE VISUAL COLLAGE IMAGE containing my photo and the complete style analysis written INSIDE THE IMAGE.The final output must be an actual designed image, like a professional fashion magazine editorial / Pinterest fashion analysis board. Use my uploaded photo as the main photo in the collage. PRESERVE MY PHOTO EXACTLY: * Do not change my face.* Do not change my body.* Do not change my clothes.* Do not change my hairstyle.* Do not change my accessories.* Do not beautify or alter my appearance.Analyze ONLY what is visibly present in the photo. ALL TEXT INSIDE THE GENERATED IMAGE MUST BE IN PERSIAN (FARSI). MOST IMPORTANT: BE COMPLETELY HONEST I want a REAL, PROFESSIONAL and UNBIASED fashion evaluation. Do NOT flatter me. Do NOT give high scores just because the photo is of me. Do NOT be polite at the expense of accuracy. Do NOT assume that everything I am wearing looks good. Do NOT give 8, 9 or 10 automatically. Evaluate my styling according to professional fashion and styling principles, including: * Color harmony * Proportion * Balance * Silhouette * Fit * Coordination * Visual hierarchy * Accessory balance * Cohesion * Sophistication * Current fashion standards * Timeless styling principles If something looks bad, mismatched, dated, visually heavy, unnecessary, or weak, SAY SO CLEARLY. If two colors do not work well together, explicitly say that they do not work well together. If an accessory makes the outfit weaker, say so. If the proportions are unbalanced, point it out. If the outfit is average, give it an average score. If the styling is genuinely excellent, give it a high score. Every score must be EARNED and supported by the actual visual evidence in the photo. Be direct, objective and professional, with ZERO sugarcoating. COLLAGE CONTENT Create a sophisticated fashion-analysis collage with my photo as the central/main element. Inside the collage, visually include these sections: «تحلیل استایل من» «استایل کلی» Briefly identify the aesthetic of my look. «لباس» Analyze the clothing, fit, silhouette, proportions and coordination. «رنگ ها» Analyze the color palette and whether the colors actually work together. «اکسسوری» Analyze the visible accessories, bag, shoes, jewelry, glasses, scarf, etc. «مو و میکاپ» Analyze the visible hair and makeup styling and whether they fit the overall look. «تناسب و فرم» Analyze the visual proportions and balance of the outfit. «نقاط قوت» Show the 3 strongest aspects of the styling. «قابل بهبود» Show the 3 most important weaknesses or improvements needed. Then create a clearly visible score panel: «امتیاز استایل» هماهنگی لباس: X/10 هماهنگی رنگ ها: X/10 تناسب و فرم: X/10 اکسسوری: X/10 مو و میکاپ: X/10 انسجام کلی: X/10 شیک بودن: X/10 به روز بودن: X/10 At the bottom create a large visual badge: «امتیاز نهایی» ⭐ X.X / 10 Under it write one short, HONEST explanation of why the look received this score. Use arrows, circles, small annotations and detail callouts around my photo to visually point to specific styling elements. DESIGN Luxury fashion magazine High-end personal stylist report Minimal editorial collage Elegant typography Clean grid layout Sophisticated neutral background Premium fashion aesthetic Beautiful spacing Clear visual hierarchy Readable Persian typography Professional graphic design Photorealistic photo integration The collage should look intentionally designed, not like random text placed around a photo. VERY IMPORTANT: THE FINAL RESPONSE MUST BE THE GENERATED COLLAGE IMAGE ITSELF. DO NOT RETURN THE ANALYSIS AS TEXT. DO NOT RETURN A DESCRIPTION OF THE IMAGE. CREATE THE SINGLE VISUAL COLLAGE IMAGE NOW.
