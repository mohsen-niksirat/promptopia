پرامپت فارسی

Transform the subject from the uploaded photo into a cute hand-drawn crayon illustration in a children's storybook style with soft anime-esque influence. Soft pastel colors and warm tones, visible textured crayon strokes, gentle shading, subtle pencil outlines, simplified proportions. Keep the anime charm with big expressive rounded eyes, subtle blush, and soft cute expressions, while still retaining the cozy whimsical crayon aesthetic. Clean white or beige background with minimal or no props to avoid distraction. Keep the subject's original facial features and proportions intact and recognizable (eyes, nose, mouth, jawline, hairstyle, skin tone), preserving identity while only stylizing with crayon texture and anime-inspired cuteness. Negative Prompt No hyper-realism, no digital sharpness, no full 3D/CGl, no watercolor/ oil/ink painting styles, no harsh manga line art, no exaggerated cartoon distortion, no extra objects, no text or logos/ watermarks, no altered pose or clothing, no changed facial structure or age, no glitches, no creepy or eerie mood, no cluttered backgrounds.
