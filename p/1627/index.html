پرامپت فارسی

Take these two photos: one of a male and one of a female. Merge their facial features, genetics, and ethnic characteristics realistically to generate two separate high-quality, photorealistic portraits: 1. A 20-year-old son – male, showing a balanced mix of both parents’ features, natural skin texture, realistic hair, and neutral expression. The son should look like a believable child of these two individuals. 2. A 20-year-old daughter – female, also showing a balanced blend of both parents, with realistic lighting, natural facial proportions, and subtle feminine traits. She should clearly look like the biological daughter of the same two parents. Both images should have clean backgrounds, natural lighting, and studio portrait quality
