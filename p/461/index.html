شخصیت شاهنامهPersian Epic Poster From Your Photo

پرامپتوپیا · گالری پرامپت‌های آماده هوش مصنوعی

پرامپت فارسی

ایجاد تصویر Create a cinematic Persian epic poster from the uploaded photo using the SAME person as the main subject. Preserve the person’s facial identity closely: face shape, eyes, nose, lips, skin tone, age, expression, and overall likeness. Do not significantly change the face. First analyze the person’s facial features and vibe, then choose the most suitable Shahnameh-inspired role for them, such as پهلوان، شاهزاده، ملکه، بانوی اشرافی، جنگجوی نجیب، قهرمان مرموز، پیام آور دربار، یا شاعر دربار. Show the same person in the final image wearing clothing appropriate to that Shahnameh-era role, with a majestic Persian background such as a palace, royal court, battlefield, ancient garden, or mythic landscape. Style should be luxurious, cinematic, realistic, Persian epic, and highly detailed. Add elegant Persian text inside the image: «اگر در شاهنامه بودی، این شخصیت می شدی: [نقش انتخابی]» Also include: «تحلیل چهره تو:» with 3–5 short Persian bullet points about the person’s facial vibe and visible features, and: «چرا این شخصیت شدی؟» with 2–4 short Persian lines explaining why that role suits the person’s face and aura. Also add 1–2 poetic Persian lines. The final image should look like a premium shareable poster with beautiful Persian typography.
