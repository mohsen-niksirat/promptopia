Sunflowers placed prominently in the foreground. Background softly blurred but still recognizable. Natural candid composition. Peaceful everyday lifestyle aesthetic. Photography Style Ultra photorealistic. Documentary lifestyle photography. Japanese and Korean aesthetic. Minimalist composition. Fujifilm GFX 100 II. 80mm lens. f/2.2 aperture.
