پرامپت فارسی

Subject: A vertical 2:3 aspect ratio Japanese street portrait titled "Untangling Shadows". A young woman stands slightly to the right of the center in a narrow, vintage Japanese alleyway lined with old wooden shopfronts. Framing is a medium-three-quarter shot (from the top of her head to around mid-thigh), with the subject occupying roughly 55% of the frame. The scene heavily features the dark wall in the background and a strong diagonal beam of sunlight pouring in from the top-right. Model & Expression: A young woman with bright ivory-beige skin featuring a natural complexion and delicate skin texture. Subtle rose blush, champagne-beige and pink-brown eye makeup, thin long eyelashes, natural lower-eyelid highlights (aegyosal), soft light eyebrows, and sheer pink-beige lips. Her expression is quiet, calm, and slightly contemplative; her face is turned slightly towards the left, with her gaze directed into the background to the far left. A subtle, tiny catchlight in her pupils. Outfit & Pose: She wears a light white sleeveless cotton blouse featuring delicate pintucks and subtle lace detailing, revealing her shoulders and collarbones in a refreshing summer aesthetic. She pairs it with a light blue high-waisted flared skirt decorated with small floral patterns. Her right hand is raised near her temple, delicately adjusting her hair with her fingertips. Her right elbow is bent outward, and her left arm rests naturally at her side. Her weight is slightly shifted to one leg, with relaxed shoulders. Background & Lighting: An old Showa-era Japanese wooden shopping street. Faded wooden planks, peeled paper signs, vintage store signboards, and blurred, illegible Japanese-style posters adorn the background. Deep shadows cover the foreground and the left side of the frame. Sharp, warm-toned sunlight strikes her face, shoulder, chest, and right arm from the top-right, creating a strong diagonal triangular shadow across her back and the bottom-left area. Soft brown reflected light warms her skin from the wooden walls. Composition & Camera: Eye-level shot taken slightly lower than chest height, using a 50mm lens equivalent at a distance of approximately 2.5 meters. The subject is positioned slightly right of center, leaving generous dark space at the top and left. A strong diagonal contrast separates the bright wall on the top-right from the deep shadows on the left. Crisp focus on her face and right hand, with a soft background blur that still retains the texture of the wood and paper. Texture & Aesthetic: Realistic Japanese fashion portrait film style. Natural skin texture, individual hair strands visible, milk tea ash brown hair with subtle fine highlights, thin see-through bangs, face-framing layers, shoulder-length hair curving gently inwards with soft flyaways catching the light. Low-saturation color palette focused on warm browns, ivory, and soft blue, finished with fine film grain and deep blacks.
