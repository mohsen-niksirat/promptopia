پرامپت فارسی

Without changing her face, make a 8K hyper-realistic 9-frame collage aesthetic photo of a 4-year-old girl in a soft minimalist editorial concept inspired by playful cardboard storytelling. The entire setup uses a warm neutral palette with creamy whites, beige, and soft brown tones. Outfit: She wears a soft cream oversized t-shirt tucked into high-waisted warm brown bloomer shorts, paired with white socks. Hair styled in short soft bangs or mini pigtails. Accessories include subtle red blush stripes on both cheeks (hand-painted look, slightly imperfect). Main prop: multiple kraft cardboard boxes and paper bags with cut-out holes, used as wearable masks, hiding spots, and playful environments. A soft plush teddy bear appears consistently across frames as her companion. Setting: Indoor soft bed setup with white textured blanket, natural soft light, minimal shadows, clean editorial feel. The collage consists of 9 cohesive frames (story-driven, slightly imperfect compositions): 1) Full-body standing shot — she stands inside an open cardboard box, hands near face in playful surprise pose, slightly off-center. Small paper bag beside her with teddy peeking out. Headline (top corner): “Tiny World” in delicate serif + handwritten mix. 2) Sitting shot — she sits on bed wearing a cardboard box over her head (face cut-out), hands touching edges, expression curious and shy. Headline: “ABY IS SO” spaced typography, minimal editorial style. 3) Inside-box shot — she sits inside a vertical box hugging a teddy bear tightly, expression soft and calm, framing slightly tight. Headline: “BABY x BEAR” clean editorial font. 4) Back-facing playful shot — she wears box on head (no face visible), standing near teddy on another box, small doodle tear icon beside teddy. Headline: minimal dots “…..” for storytelling pause. 5) Close-up — her face popping through a torn paper bag hole, laughing widely, cheeks flushed with red paint stripes, slightly overexposed highlights. Headline: none or tiny caption near edge. 6) Side interaction — she leans forward kissing or touching teddy placed on a box, body slightly stretched, composition off-balance. Headline: “Lovable Times” in soft script font. 7) Low sitting shot — she sits partially inside a box, teddy placed above the box, composition layered vertically, slightly awkward spacing. Headline: “Good day :)” small and subtle. 8) Peek-a-boo shot — she pops out from inside a box, hands gripping edge, smiling softly, teddy placed beside. Headline: speech bubble “Hi!” playful doodle style. 9) Close-up portrait — she holds a paper bag mask with face cut-out, tongue slightly out or playful expression, framing slightly tight and imperfect. Headline: minimal or tiny caption near bottom. Expression range: shy curiosity, playful joy, laughter, soft calm, mischievous expression — natural child behavior, never posed too perfectly. Shot in casual smartphone style blended with editorial softness: slight imperfections, soft focus edges, natural framing mistakes, authentic candid feel. Color grading: dreamy neutral aesthetic Lightroom preset — creamy whites, warm beige tones, soft brown shadows, lifted blacks, matte finish, gentle highlight glow, subtle skin warmth, low contrast, soft airy mood. --- Breakdown: • Lighting style: Soft natural window light, diffused, minimal shadow, warm indoor tone • Composition rule: 9-grid editorial collage, storytelling sequence, imperfect symmetry • Camera/Lens: Smartphone + soft lens simulation (slight bloom, gentle softness) • Shot angle: Mix of eye-level, slight top-down, close-up crop, intimate framing • Color grade: Dreamy neutral Lightroom preset — beige/cream dominance, matte shadows • Photographer/Aesthetic influence: Minimalist editorial meets lifestyle storytelling (inspired by Japanese kids editorial + soft magazine layout) Expert Insight: Keep prop repetition (box + teddy) across frames — this creates visual continuity and elevates the collage into a cohesive narrative rather than random shots.
