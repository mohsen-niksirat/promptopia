پرامپت فارسی

Create a cinematic 35mm analog film photo series featuring the same young adult woman with exact facial identity consistency across every frame. She has short tousled dark-brown bob hair, natural textured skin, subtle freckles, minimal makeup, soft tired eyes, and a quiet, introspective expression. She wears an oversized dark charcoal blazer over a simple dark outfit. Scene: a busy European city street during late golden hour, old urban buildings, parked dark cars, distant pedestrians and traffic, warm sunlight cutting through the streets. The world around her feels fast and restless while she remains emotionally still and detached. IMAGE 1 — SIDE PROFILE: Medium side-profile portrait of the woman standing near the street, facing left. A gentle wind blows loose strands of her short hair across her cheek. Warm sunset rim light outlines her hair and nose. She stares into the distance with a calm, melancholic expression. City architecture becomes heavily blurred behind her. IMAGE 2 — CAR WINDOW CLOSE-UP: Intimate close-up portrait photographed through a slightly dirty car window. Her face appears behind the glass in three-quarter profile, staring outside with distant, thoughtful eyes. Tiny dust particles and subtle marks are visible on the glass. Abstract red and white traffic-light bokeh glows behind her. Reflections softly overlap parts of her face. IMAGE 3 — STREET MOMENT: Candid medium-full street photograph of the same woman beside a parked black car. She turns unexpectedly toward the camera as if someone has just called her name. Wind pushes messy strands across her face. Slight motion blur in the surrounding traffic and pedestrians, spontaneous documentary composition, imperfect framing, emotionally raw. IMAGE 4 — INSIDE CAR: Extreme intimate profile close-up of the same woman sitting inside a car and looking through the side window. Her face is very close to the glass, eyes lowered slightly, lips relaxed. Small droplets, dust and imperfections on the window remain sharply visible while city lights dissolve into red, amber and teal bokeh outside. Quiet loneliness and cinematic stillness. VISUAL STYLE: 1970s–1990s European arthouse cinema, Kodak Portra 800 mixed with Cinestill 800T character, pushed 35mm film, strong organic grain, visible film noise, slightly underexposed shadows, warm amber highlights, muted teal-green shadows, faded colors, subtle halation, gentle highlight bloom, imperfect analog exposure, soft focus falloff, natural skin texture, no beauty retouching, shallow depth of field, vintage spherical lens, slight chromatic aberration, handheld documentary photography, candid editorial fashion photography, poetic urban melancholy, Wong Kar-wai-inspired emotional color atmosphere, raw photochemical texture. CAMERA: 35mm film camera, 50mm f/1.4 and 85mm f/1.8 lenses, wide aperture, available natural light only, shallow depth of field, handheld framing, subtle motion blur, authentic film scan, high ISO grain. MOOD: lonely in a crowded city, quiet longing, nostalgia, emotional distance, fleeting memory, contemplative, intimate, imperfect, cinematic. Aspect ratio 4:5 vertical. Generate as a cohesive four-image editorial photo sequence. Preserve the exact same woman, face, hairstyle, outfit and cinematic color grade in all four shots.
