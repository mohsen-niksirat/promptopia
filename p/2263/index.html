پرتره استریتویر وینتیجVintage Streetwear Portrait

پرامپتوپیا · گالری پرامپت‌های آماده هوش مصنوعی

پرامپت فارسی

Create a highly realistic vintage streetwear portrait using my uploaded photo as the identity reference. Preserve my exact facial identity, facial structure, eyes, eyebrows, nose, lips, jawline, skin tone, beard pattern, hair characteristics, and natural proportions. Keep me clearly recognizable and do not change my identity. A stylish young man photographed in a close-up three-quarter profile, looking slightly downward and toward the side. His face is partially hidden by the shadow of his baseball cap, creating a mysterious and effortless expression. He is wearing a black structured baseball cap with a curved brim pulled low over his forehead and eyes. The cap has a small minimalist white embroidered text graphic on the front. He is wearing an oversized clean white hoodie with a soft cotton texture. The hood rests naturally around the neck and shoulders. One hand is raised behind his head, casually adjusting or holding the back of his cap. His hand is relaxed and natural, with a few simple silver rings and a minimal bracelet visible on his wrist. He has natural short dark hair and a neatly trimmed beard with realistic individual facial-hair texture. Strong direct sunlight enters from one side, creating dramatic hard-edged shadows across his face, cap, hoodie and background. The sunlight produces warm highlights on the skin while the cap creates a deep shadow over the eyes. Minimal plain warm light-gray/beige wall in the background with strong architectural sunlight and shadow patterns. No distracting objects. Aesthetic of vintage film photography, contemporary streetwear editorial, understated masculine fashion, effortless cool, candid Instagram photography. Warm slightly faded color palette, muted whites, beige and brown tones, deep natural shadows, subtle reddish skin highlights, analog film grain, slight photographic texture, soft imperfections. Realistic skin pores, natural facial hair, detailed cotton fabric, realistic hands and fingers, authentic shadows, natural anatomy. Shot with a professional full-frame camera, 50mm portrait lens, close camera distance, shallow depth of field, natural optical rendering, subtle film grain, slightly faded analog color grading. Vertical 4:5 composition, close-up portrait, subject filling most of the frame, slightly off-center composition.
