کاپل وینتیجCouple Vintage Cinematic

پرامپتوپیا · گالری پرامپت‌های آماده هوش مصنوعی

پرامپت فارسی

Create a cinematic vintage couple portrait using the uploaded reference images as the ONLY identity source. Preserve both people’s exact identities from the reference photos: - same facial features - same skin texture - same hairstyle - same age appearance - same body proportions Do not alter, beautify, stylize, or redesign their faces. Scene: A nostalgic 1970s Iranian countryside forest scene. A young couple sitting together on a traditional Persian rug under a large old tree in a quiet green forest. A vintage hookah (shisha) is placed naturally beside them on the rug, creating a warm nostalgic picnic atmosphere. Composition: Vertical 4:5 fashion editorial photograph. Both people sitting close together on the ground. The woman sits on the left, holding the hookah hose naturally near her face. The man sits beside her on the right, relaxed, looking slightly downward toward her. Their poses should feel candid and natural, like a real old family photograph, not a staged pose. Clothing: Woman: - long brown vintage coat/dress - dark black headscarf - modest classic 1970s style - natural fabric texture Man: - gray vintage tailored suit - retro patterned shirt underneath - classic leather shoes - old-fashioned elegant style Environment: Dense green forest, large tree trunk, soft sunlight passing through leaves, natural shadows, Persian picnic atmosphere. Photography style: Luxury vintage fashion editorial inspired by old Dior campaigns. 1970s analog film photography. Shot on 35mm film camera. Soft film grain. Muted colors. Warm beige, olive green, brown and gray color palette. Slightly faded vintage tones. Lighting: Golden afternoon sunlight. Soft cinematic shadows. Natural depth of field. Realistic skin texture and imperfections. Make the image look like a real 1970s film photograph, nostalgic, emotional, elegant, and completely realistic. No AI look.
