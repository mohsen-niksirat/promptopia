A cinematic beach scene at dusk, just after sunset, with the sun approximately 98% below the horizon, casting a rich purple and deep red gradient across the sky. The environment is low light, nearing darkness. The subject is positioned with their back initially facing the camera, captured mid-turn as they look back over their shoulder toward the lens. The photo is taken using a direct camera flash, creating a high-contrast look where the subject is brightly illuminated against the darker background. The subject’s face has a subtle glittery, glowing effect due to the flash reflection. The subject is wearing a sky blue shirt. Composition is a medium shot framed from above the waist, focusing on the upper body. The overall style should feel natural, candid, and slightly cinematic with a sharp subject and softly dimmed background.
