پرامپت فارسی

Reference image: conceptual variant. A candid, slightly chaotic flash photograph of a couple inside a car at night, capturing a spontaneous intimate moment. Pose: both subjects are seated close together inside the car interior. The man leans forward slightly, bringing the woman's hand toward his lips as if kissing it. The woman turns her upper body toward the camera while her head is slightly tilted downward and forward, partially hiding behind her shoulder. Her eyes look directly into the lens, creating a playful, intimate tension. Their bodies are close, almost overlapping. Hairstyle: hair is loose and natural with soft volume, slightly tousled, falling freely around the face and shoulders with subtle movement. Clothing: the woman wears an oversized t-shirt with dropped shoulders, loose sleeves, and soft fabric folds. The man wears a relaxed oversized t-shirt and a structured baseball cap. Both outfits are casual, street-style inspired. Camera: shot on a wide smartphone lens (approx. 24–28mm equivalent), handheld, slightly tilted framing. Close proximity to subjects creates distortion and intimacy. Lighting: harsh direct flash from the camera creates blown highlights on skin and fabric, strong shadows behind subjects, and a slightly overexposed look. Background remains darker, with visible car interior elements like ceiling console and seats. Environment: inside a car at night, visible roof panel, rearview area, and dark windows. Ambient outside light is minimal, focus remains on flash-lit subjects.
