پرامپت فارسی

Use the uploaded reference image as the ONLY identity reference for the person. Preserve the person's exact identity and facial features with maximum fidelity. The person can be male or female depending on the reference image. Do NOT change, feminize, masculinize, beautify, reshape, or replace the face. Keep the exact facial proportions, face shape, eyes, eyebrows, nose, lips, jawline, cheekbones, skin tone, hairline, and all natural facial characteristics of the reference person. Recreate the visual style and composition of the reference scene: Ultra-realistic cinematic close-up portrait, vertical composition, head and upper shoulders filling the frame. The subject is looking slightly toward the camera with a calm, mysterious, introspective expression. Very dark environment and deep black background, with most of the face naturally falling into shadow. A narrow beam of warm golden sunlight passes through a small opening or window and creates dramatic geometric streaks of light across the face, especially around the eyes, nose and cheek. Strong chiaroscuro lighting, extremely high contrast between illuminated skin and deep shadows. The sunlight should create natural irregular shapes and sharp luminous edges across the face. Warm amber and golden highlights, subtle brown tones, realistic skin texture, visible natural pores and tiny imperfections, realistic eyelashes and individual hair strands. Soft cinematic grain, slightly dreamy analog-film atmosphere, intimate editorial photography, low-key lighting, moody and mysterious aesthetic. Keep the person's original hairstyle and general appearance from the reference image. Do not add accessories or change clothing unless necessary. Natural realistic skin, no excessive retouching, no plastic skin. 85mm portrait lens look, shallow depth of field, realistic optical falloff, subtle film grain, soft focus in darker areas, highly detailed illuminated areas, photorealistic photography. IMPORTANT: The uploaded person's identity must remain unchanged. Do not generate a different person. Do not alter gender. Do not modify facial proportions. Do not make the person conventionally prettier. Only recreate the lighting, composition, atmosphere, camera perspective and photographic aesthetic of the reference.
