پرامپت فارسی

A realistic photo in iPhone POV style, vertical 9:16, natural quality with no filters, with realistic nighttime lighting outside a Shell / Super7 / convenience store. I'm sitting at a metal table outside the store, looking sad, talking with the cat from the photo I uploaded. Around me, cats of different breeds are gathered, acting as if they're comforting me and talking with me. The bright white fluorescent lights from the store create strong shadows and realistic reflections. Behind us, there are large windows filled with snacks and drinks. On one side, a dark street with parked motorcycles is visible. On the table there are chips, bottles of tea, and instant noodle cups. The cats have sad and concerned expressions, looking at me instead of the camera, in a spontaneous candid style. A hyper-realistic blend of a real human and cats, naturally integrated into the scene.
