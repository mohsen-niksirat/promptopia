پرامپت فارسی

A photorealistic vertical shot 9:16, an extremely large macro portrait of a girl with a feminine look. Her face is almost completely hidden by a soft light-gray knit hoodie, and only one eye is visible in the lower left corner of the frame through a narrow slit. The eye is in sharp focus, with a wet shine on the iris, very long expressive black eyelashes, and straight hair partially hanging over the eyelid. Her gaze is directed at the camera, creating a sense of secrecy, intimacy, and a nighttime selfie. On the right in the foreground, fingers are pulling the hoodie's sleeve, and a short square manicure with a French tip is visible; the nail closer to the lens is slightly softer in focus but retains a smooth shine. The composition is tight and cropped, with fabric stripes running horizontally from the top and diagonally around the face, creating graphic layers and strong overlaps. The shallow depth of field emphasizes the eyelashes, the texture of the knit, and the nail. Photorealism, photo taken with a Canon G7X, no perfect symmetry, visible skin texture, pores, raw photo, film grain, no "staged" feeling, dark photo, harsh flash effect, graininess, photo 3:4
