پرامپت فارسی

A soft cinematic outdoor photoshoot of a young woman in a grassy field during golden hour. She has long wavy brown hair, natural makeup, and a calm, dreamy expression. She is wearing an oversized grey knitted sweater and black pants. Multiple compositions: close-up portrait with hand near face, seated pose on grass with one arm raised over head, standing full-body shot with relaxed posture, and a mid-shot holding sweater slightly. Lighting is warm, diffused, and natural with soft shadows. Background is an open field with muted green tones and shallow depth of field (bokeh). The mood is cozy, minimal, and aesthetic. Shot on DSLR, 85mm lens, f/1.8, high detail, cinematic color grading, earthy tones, soft focus, editorial fashion photography style.
