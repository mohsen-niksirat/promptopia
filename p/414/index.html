پرتره نور طلاییEyes Photo Fall

پرامپتوپیا · گالری پرامپت‌های آماده هوش مصنوعی

پرامپت فارسی

Create image: A hyper-photorealistic bedroom portrait of the same female subject in the attached image, preserving exact facial features and natural asymmetry with full realism. SCENE: Subject standing sideways near a bedroom wall during late-afternoon golden hour sunlight. One arm lifted above head holding messy curly bun, exposing shoulder, collarbone, and upper back. She turns her head back toward the camera with relaxed soft gaze and soft parted lips. The pose should feel accidental and naturally caught. CAMERA / SMARTPHONE LOGIC: Shot on an iPhone 15 Pro rear camera. Ultra-sharp smartphone digital clarity with subtle computational processing artifacts. No DSLR blur. No cinematic grading. Visible smartphone characteristics: slight HDR flattening in shadows, minor edge sharpening around curls and tank top seams, subtle skin smoothing only in bright highlights, slight exposure clipping on shoulder and cheekbone, mild warm sensor bloom from direct sunset light, tiny JPEG softness in darker background areas, natural handheld micro-tilt, slight highlight haze around arm edges from strong sunlight. Background: Soft bedroom interior with shelf decor, warm beige wall, soft shadow gradients, faint room texture still readable without heavy blur. LIGHTING: Strong warm golden-hour sunlight entering from the right side window creating dramatic rectangular sunlight geometry across wall and body. Realistic exposure imbalance typical of smartphone HDR struggling between bright sunlight and dark interior. SKIN / TEXTURE: Rosy complexion with peach-pink undertones, authentic pores, faint freckles, natural flushed cheeks, realistic under-eye depth, glossy lips with uneven natural shine, subtle skin sheen from heat and sunlight. Skin must retain texture even in bright highlights. HAIR: Messy curly updo with loose ringlets and humid flyaways. Some curls sharply rim-lit by sunlight while deeper curls fall into shadow. Slight frizz and uneven strand separation from natural texture and heat. OUTFIT: White ribbed tank top with realistic fabric tension and stretch around torso. Ribbed texture visible in highlights. Slight folding and compression near underarm and waist from pose pressure. COMPOSITION: Vertical 4:5 framing. Subject occupies around 70% of frame. Face positioned upper-right third. Raised arm creates strong diagonal geometry across top frame. Phone shadow creates secondary geometric shape on torso and wall. Crop should feel intimate and slightly imperfect like a spontaneous bedroom photo.
