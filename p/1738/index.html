پرامپت فارسی

Use the uploaded reference faces as the ONLY identity source for both people. ABSOLUTE FACE LOCK (BOTH): Preserve each person's identity 100% exactly. Do not change face shape, forehead, hairline, eyebrows, eyebrow density, eye shape, eye spacing, eyelids, eyelashes, iris color, nose, lips, jawline, skin tone, skin texture, pores, beard, age, or natural facial proportions. No beautification, no AI face replacement, no resemblance drift. COMPOSITION: Ultra-realistic black-and-white cinematic portrait. Vertical 4:5. Extreme close-up. Both faces occupy almost the entire frame. The woman is on the left. The man is on the right. Their faces are pressed tightly together so their temples and cheeks touch naturally at the center. Both look directly into the camera. Only half of each face is visible, creating a perfectly balanced split composition. Show one full eye from each person and include the complete nose and lips, similar to a symmetrical half-face portrait. Crop just below the lips and slightly above the forehead. No shoulders, no hands, no distracting background. LIGHTING: Soft cinematic studio lighting. High-contrast monochrome. Natural skin texture. Visible pores. Fine facial hair. Individual eyebrow hairs. Natural eyelashes. Realistic beard texture. Sharp eyes with subtle catchlights. EXPRESSION: Neutral, calm, intimate, emotional. No smile. Relaxed lips. BACKGROUND: Pure black or very dark seamless studio background. STYLE: Ultra-photorealistic. 85mm portrait lens. f/2. 8K. Professional editorial photography. Natural film-like contrast.
