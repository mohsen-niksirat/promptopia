Reproduce the lighting pattern of the target reference precisely: * strong directional key light * warm natural skin illumination * deep shadow across the opposite side of the face * dramatic chiaroscuro effect * subtle reflected light inside the shadows * realistic specular highlights on the skin * natural falloff across the face * dark neutral environment The lighting must shape the person's **existing facial structure, not modify it. ### EXPRESSION Keep the expression subtle, calm, confident, and slightly mysterious. Natural relaxed lips. Focused eyes. No exaggerated smile. No exaggerated emotion. The expression should feel like an authentic high-end editorial photograph. ### COMPOSITION Maintain the target composition: * vertical portrait * extremely close framing * face dominant in the frame * eyes positioned naturally within the upper-middle area * slight head tilt * intimate camera distance * minimal visible background * subject centered slightly toward one side * strong shadow/light division across the face Do not introduce additional people, objects, text, logos, props, or environmental elements. ### COLOR & FINISH Natural warm skin tones. Deep neutral blacks. Subtle warm highlights. Muted cinematic color grading. High-end fashion editorial finish. Realistic contrast. No oversaturated colors. No artificial HDR. No excessive orange skin. No heavy retouching. ### ABSOLUTE RESTRICTIONS Do NOT: * change the person's identity * alter facial proportions * reshape the nose * reshape the lips * enlarge the eyes * modify the jawline * change the cheekbones * artificially smooth the skin * make the person conventionally more attractive * change their age * change their natural hair characteristics * replace their facial features with another person's features * copy the user's original photo's pose * copy the user's original photo's background * copy the user's original photo's lighting * copy the user's original photo's composition * copy the user's original photo's clothing The uploaded image is ONLY the identity/appearance reference. Everything else must be derived from the target scene described above. ### FINAL QUALITY REQUIREMENT The result must look like a real photograph of the exact person from the uploaded reference, photographed in the target scene by a professional fashion photographer. Prioritize in this order: 1. Identity fidelity 2. Facial structure fidelity 3. Hair fidelity 4. Target composition 5. Lighting accuracy 6. Photorealism 7. Editorial aesthetic No visible AI artifacts. No face blending. No identity drift. No generic-model substitution.
