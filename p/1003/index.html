A close-up selfie of a young woman outdoors, taken from a slightly low angle, like a live smartphone selfie, from roughly the top of her stomach upwards. Her face is positioned closer to the upper center, her head tilted slightly toward the camera, her gaze directed downwards into the lens, her expression soft and calm, with a subtle smile. Her face has a light skin tone, dark, expressive eyebrows, almond-shaped eyes with neat makeup, and a natural expression, without the need for studio posing. Her long, thick hair, lifted by a strong wind, becomes the main movement in the frame: thin strands arc across the entire foreground, crossing her forehead, cheek, and lips, partially obscuring one eye, creating highlights in the light. The clothing is light and summery: a loose white textured shirt is open over a white top, the fabric brightly backlit by the sun, with soft folds and shadows. Jewelry is minimal, with a small light-colored earring visible near the ear. The background is completely open: a rich blue sky with thin cirrus clouds, without a horizon, buildings, or unnecessary objects. The lighting is harsh, daylight, direct, from above and from the side, creating clean contrasts, shimmering hair edges, and a fresh summer palette of white and sky blue.
