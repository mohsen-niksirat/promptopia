Ultra-realistic close-up beauty portrait, editorial photography style. Same face, skin texture, natural hair color and texture, and exact eye color as in the reference photo provided — preserve facial identity and likeness precisely, photorealistic, not airbrushed, natural skin pores and subtle imperfections visible. Pose: subject holds the collar of a dark navy ribbed turtleneck sweater up to her chin/mouth with both hands, fingers curled around the fabric near her cheeks, elbows raised. Nails painted deep navy blue, one hand wearing a chunky silver ring. A single large blue morpho butterfly rests over one eye/eyebrow, wings open, as a surreal styling element. Makeup: sharp winged blue eyeliner on the visible eye, matching blue-toned eyeshadow, natural glowing skin, soft contour, neutral lips. Hair: soft tousled waves, natural volume, loose strands falling over forehead, visible individual hair strands with realistic shine and flyaways. Lighting: soft diffused studio lighting, gentle shadows, catchlight in the eyes, cool-toned color grading with a muted blue-grey background. Camera: shot on 85mm portrait lens, shallow depth of field, sharp focus on eyes and face, subject centered, close crop from chest up. Extremely high detail, 8k quality, realistic skin rendering, no plastic/CGI look, professional editorial fashion photography.
