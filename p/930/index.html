Ultra realistic cinematic countryside full body photography, shot on Canon PowerShot G7X Mark III, authentic real life outdoor photo. STRICT FULL BODY REFERENCE LOCK 1:1. FRAMING LOCK: FULL BODY PHOTO. Show the entire woman from head to toes. Visible head scarf, full dress, legs, socks and complete boots. Do NOT crop the feet. Do NOT crop the legs. Camera placed farther away. Vertical full body fashion shot. Woman takes about 40% of the frame. Show large dramatic sky and countryside around her. CAMERA ANGLE: Camera behind the woman at full body distance. Slightly lower angle. Same walking-away perspective as reference. Keep the dirt road leading into the distance. POSE: Woman walking away from camera. Back mostly turned. Body slightly rotated to show side profile. Head looking down over shoulder. One leg forward in walking motion. One hand holding the white ruffle dress. Other hand holding wicker basket with dried flowers. OUTFIT: White romantic layered mini dress with lace ruffles. White head scarf / bandana. Black tall rubber boots. White socks visible above boots. SCENE: Golden wheat field. Country dirt road. Huge dark dramatic storm clouds. Windy before-rain atmosphere. HAIR: Use client’s real hair color and length. Hair moving naturally in the wind under the scarf. STYLE: Canon G7X realistic photo. Natural outdoor details. Sharp subject. Slight grain. No AI look. IMPORTANT: FULL BODY ONLY. Feet and boots must be visible. Do not zoom into face. Do not create portrait. Keep exact reference composition. --ar 9:16 --style raw
