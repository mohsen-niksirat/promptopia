پرامپت فارسی

Create a faithful vertical 2:3 monochrome studio portrait matching the reference image: a young adult woman shown from the upper chest upward, nearly front-facing, looking directly into the camera with a calm, slightly wistful expression. Preserve her softly oval face, large light almond-shaped eyes, naturally arched dark brows, slender straight nose, softly defined lips, delicate jawline, light skin, and distinctive facial proportions. Reproduce her dark wavy hair in voluminous curls, the wide woven straw hat with a small cluster of roses on the viewer-left, pearl drop earrings, layered necklaces with an ornate teardrop pendant, and the pale high-neck lace blouse with ruffles, pleats, scalloped trim, and puffed sleeves. Use an eye-level close portrait, likely 85mm lens, shallow depth of field, sharp focus on the eyes, soft gradual falloff, dark blurred studio background, broad diffused light from slightly above viewer-left, gentle shadows, soft catchlights, warm sepia black-and-white tones, creamy highlights, rich midtones, subtle vignette, fine film grain, realistic skin and textile textures, elegant historical studio photography, quiet romantic mood. Avoid identity drift, altered facial features, modern styling, extra objects, bad anatomy, plastic skin, harsh lighting, oversharpening, text, logos, and watermarks
