Ultra-realistic vertical 9:16 street portrait. Preserve the girl’s face, hair, and identity exactly as the reference. Natural asymmetric pose, one leg slightly forward. Strong vivid pink color theme throughout the image and alley, with rich pink tones dominating the atmosphere. She holds a small cake with one satin pink bow and one white lit candle, no text.
