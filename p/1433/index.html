Transform the exact same person from the reference image into their realistic 70-year-old version. Preserve the person's identity with maximum facial fidelity: same face structure, same eyes, same eye shape, same nose, same lips, same facial proportions, same unique characteristics. Do not create a different elderly person. Do not replace the face. The person must remain clearly recognizable as the same individual. Maintain: - original skin tone - original eye color - original hair color and hair length reference - original facial identity Only apply realistic aging changes. AGE TRANSFORMATION Make the person approximately 70 years old. Natural aging: realistic wrinkles around eyes and forehead, natural skin texture, subtle age spots, realistic mature skin, natural gray or white hair according to the reference, healthy and elegant elderly appearance. No exaggerated aging. No artificial old-person makeup. No plastic skin. No beauty filter. POSE AND EMOTION Create this exact intimate elderly couple pose: An elderly woman is sitting behind an elderly man on a dark leather sofa. The woman gently hugs the man from behind. Her arms are wrapped around him in a natural loving embrace. Her head is softly resting against the man's head. Both have their eyes closed. Both have a peaceful, emotional, gentle smile. Their hands are placed together in front of the man's chest: the man's hands are naturally held and supported by the woman's hands. Accurate hand anatomy: exactly two hands for each person, no extra fingers, no duplicated hands, natural finger placement. The pose should express decades of love, comfort, and companionship. CLOTHING AND DETAILS Woman: wearing a white short-sleeve blouse with black floral and leaf patterns, a single-strand pearl necklace, a metallic bracelet on her right wrist. Man: wearing a light blue-gray short-sleeve button-up shirt, a simple wedding ring visible on his right ring finger. ENVIRONMENT They are sitting on a dark leather sofa. Warm home interior background: soft light-colored textured plaster wall, cream or off-white tones, a dark wooden door frame visible on the right side. Soft natural window light coming from the left side. Warm natural color palette. Background completely blurred with shallow depth of field and soft bokeh. PHOTOGRAPHY STYLE Medium close-up documentary portrait photography. Eye-level camera angle. Central composition. The couple occupies the main frame. Ultra realistic photorealistic image. Professional camera quality. Natural skin pores. Detailed hair strands. Realistic fabric texture. Soft cinematic lighting. Natural film grain. 35mm documentary photography style. No lens distortion. Natural perspective. Accurate anatomy.
