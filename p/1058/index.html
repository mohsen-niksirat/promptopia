پرامپت فارسی

A natural café portrait of a girl sitting deep in a chair with legs comfortably apart, holding an iced coffee near her lips with her left hand while her right hand lightly adjusts her sunglasses. Relaxed posture, leaning back into the chair, head slightly turned to the side, looking away from the camera. Modern outdoor café atmosphere, casual summer outfit, candid lifestyle photography, realistic skin texture, authentic daylight, natural colors. Preserve facial identity.

English prompt

A natural café portrait of a girl sitting comfortably in a chair with one arm resting on the chair arm and the other holding a smartphone. Legs crossed, body angled slightly away from the camera, head turned sideways as if watching something outside the frame. Relaxed urban café vibe, effortless posture, realistic daylight, authentic environment, natural skin texture, candid lifestyle photo. Preserve facial identity.
