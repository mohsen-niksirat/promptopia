پرامپت فارسی

Turn the uploaded photo into a very funny, high-quality caricature. Exaggerate the most distinctive facial features in a cute and playful way while keeping the person recognizable. Add creative cartoon elements, props, animals, objects, and visual jokes that match the person’s appearance, expression, outfit, mood, interests, and overall vibe, making the scene richer and funnier. Use a colorful, detailed, professional caricature style with strong personality and storytelling. Add a short humorous Persian caption that fits the photo’s vibe, written in authentic Persian handwritten calligraphy style. The caption should be witty, unique to the image, and naturally integrated into the artwork.
