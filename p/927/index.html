مدل مو سینماییPalm Portrait Cinematic

پرامپتوپیا · گالری پرامپت‌های آماده هوش مصنوعی

پرامپت فارسی

Use the uploaded portrait as the strict identity reference for the character’s face. Preserve the exact facial identity from the uploaded image, including facial structure, eyes, eyebrows, nose, lips, jawline, ears, skin tone, skin texture, hairstyle, hairline, hair color, facial hair if present, age impression, and overall recognizable likeness. Do not beautify, stylize, change, regenerate, or reinterpret the face. Only adapt the face naturally to the new lighting, shadows, camera angle, haze, and cinematic atmosphere. A low-angle, close-up shot captures the same person from the uploaded image sitting in a dimly lit setting, partially obscured by large yellow-orange flames dominating the right side of the frame. The character is dressed in a relaxed, patterned light-colored shirt with prominent buttons and leans back with a languid posture, holding a cigarette near the mouth with one hand and a drink or small object with the other. The face must remain identical to the uploaded portrait, but it is partially obscured by shadows and haze, illuminated by flickering firelight that casts dramatic warm highlights and diffuse shadows across the features while enhancing the existing facial structure with cinematic sharpness. The background is a deep, nearly black, indistinct space, reinforcing a sense of isolation. The color palette focuses on earthy browns, ochres, and dark sepias, with the fire’s orange glow as the main accent. The image should feel moody, gritty, introspective, intimate, and intense. The overall softness, muted colors, slight haze, film grain, and post-production texture should suggest a still from a digital cinematic film. Keep the uploaded person’s face recognizable and unchanged. Do not replace the face with a new model. Do not change the facial proportions, expression identity, hairstyle, age, or defining facial details. Only change the pose, clothing, lighting, environment, and cinematic firelit atmosphere.
