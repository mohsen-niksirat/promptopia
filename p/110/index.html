کلاژ سیاه‌وسفیدPalm Collage Studio

پرامپتوپیا · گالری پرامپت‌های آماده هوش مصنوعی

پرامپت فارسی

Create an ultra-realistic 3×3 editorial lifestyle collage featuring the same mature male portrayed (uploaded photo) in nine different imaginative professions, hobbies, and dream scenarios. Each panel should seamlessly blend real photography with hand-drawn black ink doodle illustrations, creating the illusion that simple sketch art comes to life around the subject. The collage should have a minimalist white studio background with soft diffused lighting and subtle shadows. Every frame is separated by thin white borders, maintaining a clean magazine-style grid layout. The photographic subject remains perfectly consistent across all nine scenes with identical facial identity, skin tone, hairstyle, sunglasses, and natural proportions. Character Consistency Same adult male in every frame Happy facial expression Ultra-realistic skin texture Natural facial proportions Consistent identity throughout Professional DSLR quality Sharp focus 8K ultra-high resolution Photorealistic rendering Panel 1 – Surfing Adventure The subject rides a surfboard in an energetic surfing pose while balancing naturally. Massive ocean waves, splashing water, foam, and motion lines are illustrated as expressive black ink sketches surrounding him. The board is partially realistic while integrated into the doodle wave. Panel 2 – Astronaut in Space The subject wears a realistic NASA-inspired astronaut suit, floating weightlessly in space. Hand-drawn doodles include stars, planets, the moon, and a launching rocket beneath him. His body appears to drift naturally in zero gravity. Panel 3 – Reading on Clouds The subject lies comfortably on fluffy white clouds reading an open hardcover book. Behind him are sketched bookshelves, desk lamp, books, and cozy study-room doodles. The clouds blend realistically with the illustrated environment. Panel 4 – Scientist The subject wears a clean white laboratory coat while examining a glass test tube. Around him are hand-drawn laboratory flasks, beakers, molecular structures, chemical formulas, bubbles, and a comic-style speech bubble reading "EUREKA!" Panel 5 – Gardener The subject kneels beside a raised wooden garden bed watering fresh vegetables with a watering can. Illustrated herbs, leaves, flowers, gardening tools, insects, and botanical sketches surround the realistic garden. Panel 6 – Meditation The subject sits cross-legged in a peaceful lotus meditation pose. Beneath him is a large illustrated lotus flower while glowing spiritual mandala lines, aura circles, and zen-inspired doodles radiate behind him, creating a tranquil atmosphere. Panel 7 – Chef The subject wears a chef's hat and apron while holding a wooden spoon beside a steaming tagine cooking pot. Hand-drawn vegetables, herbs, garlic, tomatoes, zucchini, steam curls, and kitchen doodles decorate the composition. Panel 8 – Carpenter The subject wears protective gloves and safety glasses while carefully building a small wooden cabinet using a hammer. The workbench contains realistic woodworking tools, while additional rulers, screws, nails, saws, and construction diagrams are illustrated around him. Panel 9 – Artist The subject wears a paint-splattered artist apron while holding a paintbrush and wooden palette. A realistic easel stands beside him displaying a partially painted landscape. Hand-drawn paint splashes, brushes, tubes, creative doodles, and artistic sketch lines surround the workspace.
