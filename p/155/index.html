پرامپت فارسی

Use my photo as the base. Photorealistic photograph in 9:16 format. MAKEUP: Dewy, luminous skin with even tone and prominent freckles. Subtle highlighter on the inner corners of the eyes, cheekbones, and bridge of the nose. Long winged eyeliner extending toward the temples, paired with anime-style (manga lashes) individual false eyelashes. MANICURE: Very long, pointed stiletto nails in a cool-toned milky pink nude shade with a glossy gel finish. APPEARANCE: Straight hair falling freely over the shoulders. Blue long-sleeved top. Holding a large, fluffy white-and-gray cat with bright blue eyes in her arms. POSE: Standing turned in a three-quarter view toward the camera, holding the cat in front of her at shoulder level. The woman’s face is partially hidden behind the cat’s head, with only one eye visible. Her hand rests gently on the cat’s back. BACKGROUND: Light-colored brick wall with shadows of tree branches cast across it. The edge of a white curtain is visible on the right side. LIGHTING: Bright, warm natural light. Side sunlight creates a golden highlight in the hair. COLOR SCHEME AND ATMOSPHERE: Soft pastel warm color palette with harmonious blue, white, and gold tones. Gentle contrast with high clarity and detail in the cat’s fur.
