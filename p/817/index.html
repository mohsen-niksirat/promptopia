A high-fashion editorial portrait of a poised woman as the sole hero, seated in a brushed-metal elevator with both arms lifted and black leather–gloved hands braced against the side walls, conveying icy confidence. She wears a deep espresso-brown fur stole over a dark top, oversized black wraparound sunglasses, and a black-and-ivory zebra satin pencil skirt cinched by a square crystal-framed buckle belt; her jet-black hair is sleek in a center-parted short bob tucked behind the ears, with warm light-medium beige skin and softly contoured, nude-matte lips against the cool steel panels, lit by a soft overhead/forward key with smooth falloff that sculpts cheekbones and keeps highlights controlled, shot on an 50 mm lens at f 2.8 ISO 400 1 160 s, with the model tack-sharp and the background gently blurred. Render premium micro-texture: fine skin pores without harshness, glossy glove creases, dense fur fibers, and satin sheen with crisp zebra contrast. Model description: mid-20s, ethnically ambiguous, nationality unspecified, dark eyes obscured by sunglasses, straight refined nose, defined brows, full lips, high cheekbones, sharp jawline, slim tall-leaning build. Lighting architecture: large soft key, negative fill for depth, subtle rim separation from the metal, rich blacks with retained detail. Preserve highlight roll-off on sunglasses and satin, avoid blown speculars, keep tonal depth cinematic. Compose centered and symmetrical with clean silhouettes and minimal clutter. Finish with luxe editorial grading, deep neutral shadows, refined contrast, light film-grain, and immaculate retouching for a dark, moody, high-end fashion statement.
