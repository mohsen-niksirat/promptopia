کاپل وینتیجCouple Portrait Vintage

پرامپتوپیا · گالری پرامپت‌های آماده هوش مصنوعی

پرامپت فارسی

Create a cinematic vintage couple portrait using the uploaded reference images as the ONLY identity source. Identity preservation: Keep both people’s faces exactly identical to the reference photos. Preserve facial structure, skin texture, hairstyle, age, body proportions and unique features. Do not beautify, redesign, or change their identities. Scene: A nostalgic 1970s Iranian vintage fashion photograph. A man and a woman standing together beside a classic red Iranian vintage car (Paykan style) in a quiet forest road surrounded by tall green trees. Composition: Vertical 4:5 portrait. Full body shot. The man stands leaning casually against the front of the vintage car, hands relaxed, wearing a thoughtful natural expression. The woman stands beside him slightly turned toward him, resting her chin on her hand in a calm emotional pose. Their interaction should feel candid and natural, like a real photograph captured decades ago. Clothing: Man: - gray vintage tailored suit - retro patterned shirt underneath - classic leather shoes - old leather shoulder bag Woman: - long brown vintage coat/dress - dark black headscarf - modest 1970s Iranian fashion - vintage handbag Environment: Old red classic car, forest background, natural dirt road, large trees, soft sunlight passing through leaves. Photography style: Luxury vintage fashion editorial inspired by classic Dior campaigns. 1970s analog film photography. Shot on a 35mm film camera. Soft film grain. Slightly faded colors. Warm nostalgic color grading. Muted brown, olive green, beige and gray tones. Lighting: Golden afternoon sunlight. Soft cinematic shadows. Natural depth of field. Realistic textures. Make it look like an authentic old 1970s film photograph, emotional, elegant, timeless and completely realistic. No AI look.
