پرامپت فارسی

Create a highly realistic cinematic masculine portrait using my uploaded photo as the identity reference. Preserve my exact facial identity, facial structure, eyes, eyebrows, nose, lips, jawline, skin tone, hairline, and natural proportions. Keep me clearly recognizable and do not change my identity. A stylish young man standing casually in a dark indoor or semi-indoor environment, possibly inside a parked car or a dimly lit garage. The background is almost completely dark and minimal, with only subtle environmental details visible. He is wearing a relaxed oversized light taupe/beige-gray long-sleeve shirt with a soft lightweight fabric texture. The sleeves are naturally pushed and slightly rolled up around the forearms. He wears loose dark chocolate-brown trousers. He is wearing narrow rectangular black/brown tinted sunglasses with a thin frame and a simple elegant wristwatch with a dark leather strap and metallic case. A subtle minimal chain necklace is visible around his neck. His pose is relaxed and confident: one hand is placed casually inside his trouser pocket while the other arm is raised, with his hand touching the back of his neck or adjusting his collar. His head is turned slightly to the side, looking away from the camera with a calm, confident expression. Strong warm golden sunlight enters from one side and illuminates only part of his face, neck, arms, and shirt, creating dramatic high-contrast shadows. The rest of the environment remains deep black and underexposed. Moody luxury masculine aesthetic, quiet confidence, sophisticated minimal fashion, warm brown and beige color palette, dramatic chiaroscuro lighting, deep blacks, golden highlights, natural skin texture, realistic pores, detailed hair strands, realistic fabric folds. Shot with a professional camera, close-to-medium full-body portrait, slightly low camera angle, shallow depth of field, natural lens characteristics, subtle film grain, cinematic color grading, realistic shadows and highlights, photorealistic, high detail, editorial fashion photography. Vertical 4:5 composition, natural body proportions, realistic hands and fingers, authentic candid pose.
