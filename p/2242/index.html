پرامپت فارسی

Transform ONLY the people into extremely cute hand-drawn doodle characters while keeping the entire original photographic background completely unchanged and realistic. Preserve each person’s exact position, scale, orientation, pose, gesture, hairstyle, clothing colors, and accessories. Do not add people or invent hidden faces. Style: oversized round heads, tiny bodies, simple cute faces, rosy cheeks, thin shaky black outlines, imperfect sketch lines, and messy colored-pencil/crayon fills with small white gaps. Keep correct depth, scale, ground contact, and occlusion. All background, furniture, plants, objects, lighting, colors, framing, perspective, and textures must remain photographic and untouched. Avoid: anime, manga, realistic anatomy, polished/vector art, background illustration, changed poses, or invented objects. Remove any person standing between the two main people.
