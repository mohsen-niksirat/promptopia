واترمارک سیاه‌وسفیدWoman Portrait Photo

پرامپتوپیا · گالری پرامپت‌های آماده هوش مصنوعی

پرامپت فارسی

Use the uploaded reference image as the EXACT visual and identity reference. Create ONE vertical 4:5 image containing THREE separate black-and-white photos stacked vertically like an authentic vintage contact sheet or film strip. All three frames must show the SAME adult woman from the reference. Preserve her exact facial identity, facial structure, proportions, asymmetries, eyes, eyebrows, nose, lips, cheekbones, jawline, and face shape. Do not redesign, beautify, age, de-age, swap, reconstruct, or change her face. She has long dark hair in a loose, naturally messy high bun/updo, with realistic flyaways and thin strands falling around the forehead, temples, cheeks, ears, and neck. Makeup: strong dark smoky eyes, heavy black eyeliner, dark lashes, defined brows, softly sculpted features, muted natural slightly glossy lips. Outfit: simple black top with a rounded or modest neckline, no logos, jewelry, or distracting accessories. Background: plain dark-gray interior wall, slightly textured and softly lit by direct camera flash, with an intimate, private, nostalgic, raw, mysterious mood. Photographic style: authentic late-1990s/early-2000s compact-camera or disposable-film aesthetic. High-contrast monochrome, direct on-camera flash, bright facial highlights, deep shadows, visible film grain, soft gray midtones, deep blacks, slight dust and scratches, tiny imperfections, mild blur, slight motion softness, uneven exposure, mild lens distortion, subtle vignette, faded analog tonal response, imperfect autofocus. It must look like an old personal photograph scanned from physical film, not a clean digital image, polished studio portrait, or cinematic photograph. Use a cheap compact camera look, close distance, 35–50mm equivalent perspective, no cinematic bokeh. TOP FRAME: Close-up frontal portrait, face centered and filling most of the frame. She looks directly into the camera with an intense, mysterious, slightly provocative expression. Head nearly straight with a subtle tilt. One hand is raised near her mouth, one finger gently touching or resting on her lower lip. Lips slightly parted. Dark eyes and smoky makeup are the main focus. Loose hair strands frame her face. Direct flash against a dark background. MIDDLE FRAME: Side-profile or strong three-quarter portrait of the same woman, head turned toward the right side of the frame and slightly tilted upward. She is laughing naturally, eyes partly closed, mouth open in a genuine spontaneous smile. The messy bun is clearly visible from the side, with loose strands around her face and neck. The moment must feel candid and unposed. Match the flash, grain, scratches, exposure, and film texture of the first frame. BOTTOM FRAME: Another close-up frontal portrait of the same woman, face centered similarly to the top frame. She looks directly at the camera with a calm, confident, slightly serious expression, lips softly closed or subtly parted. Keep the same identity, messy bun, loose strands, makeup, clothing, lighting, and film characteristics, with a slightly different pose from Frame 1. The mood is effortless and intimate. Composition: exactly THREE horizontal photographs stacked vertically, equal width, with no gaps or only extremely thin natural seams. No borders or decorative graphics. Maintain consistent identity, styling, camera, lighting, and environment across all frames. ABSOLUTE NEGATIVE REQUIREMENTS: no color, no modern glossy beauty photography, plastic or AI-looking skin, facial reconstruction, face swap, different woman, altered identity, exaggerated proportions, unnatural eyes, extra fingers, malformed hands, duplicated body parts, excessive sharpening, HDR, beauty filter, cinematic color grading, text, captions, watermark, borders, or graphic elements. Final result: a genuine, raw, intimate, imperfect, nostalgic three-photo black-and-white contact sheet captured spontaneously with a cheap camera and direct flash, featuring the exact same woman in every frame. size 3:4
