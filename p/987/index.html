پرامپت فارسی

Use the uploaded IMAGE 1 only as the identity reference. Create a premium vertical 4:5 digital portrait of the exact same person, shown from the chest up against a completely pure black background. STRICT IDENTITY LOCK: Preserve the person’s exact recognizable facial identity, natural facial structure, face shape, eyes, eyebrows, nose, lips, jawline, skin tone, hairstyle, hair color, age and distinctive features. Do not beautify, redesign, feminize, masculinize, widen, slim, reconstruct or replace the face. The final portrait must remain clearly recognizable as IMAGE 1. VISUAL STYLE: A sophisticated monochrome black-and-white digital illustration combining realistic portrait painting, graphic-novel shading and elegant hand-drawn ink details. Use layered charcoal-gray brush strokes, sharp black shadows, smooth grayscale highlights and subtle pencil-like texture. The result should feel artistic and illustrated while preserving realistic facial anatomy—not a photograph and not a cartoon. LIGHTING: Add an intense electric golden-yellow neon rim light around the outer edges of the hair, loose hair strands, jawline, neck, shoulders and clothing. The yellow light must create a crisp glowing silhouette against the black background, with delicate luminous lines tracing individual flyaway hairs. Keep the face primarily grayscale, with only subtle yellow light reflecting along the shadow-side contours. High contrast, dramatic cinematic lighting. COMPOSITION: Chest-up fashion portrait, subject centered, shoulders slightly angled, head subtly tilted upward, confident and calm expression, direct or slightly downward eye contact with the camera. Natural lips, relaxed posture and an editorial high-fashion mood. Preserve the reference hairstyle unless a different hairstyle is specifically requested. CLOTHING: Simple black oversized open shirt or jacket over a plain white top, rendered in deep black and grayscale with realistic folds. Do not add logos, jewelry or patterns unless visible in IMAGE 1. QUALITY: Ultra-detailed facial features, clean linework, rich black levels, refined grayscale shading, realistic anatomy, sharp expressive eyes, detailed hair strands, premium poster artwork, 4K quality. NEGATIVE INSTRUCTIONS: No Instagram interface, screenshot elements, captions, icons, numbers, watermark or text. No extra people, extra limbs, distorted face, asymmetrical eyes, plastic skin, oily skin, beauty filter, 3D render, anime style, childish cartoon style, colorful background, yellow skin, excessive glow or blurry details.
