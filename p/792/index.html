پرامپت فارسی

Use the two uploaded photos as references. Replace the model in the scene with the woman from the first reference image. Preserve her exact face, natural features, proportions and skin texture without any beautification or alteration. Do not change her facial structure, eye shape, lips, nose or natural asymmetry. The second reference image (the baby) must appear as a projected photograph on the wall behind the model. The projection should look exactly like real projector light: flat on the wall, slightly diffused, with natural fall-off and no depth. The adult model sits on the floor in the foreground in a relaxed, natural pose, with her legs bent and tucked beneath her. She wears a free-flowing, modern light-colored dress in white or cream, light and airy, and high-heeled sandals on her feet. She holds a large birthday cake with white frosting and a single lit candle, looking at it softly with a calm, gentle expression. The scene takes place in a spacious, bright studio with clean white walls and plenty of soft ambient light. The camera angle is shot slightly from above and angled downward with a flash. Lighting is warm, soft and studio-like in cream-beige tones, lluminating the model naturally while keeping the projection behind her clearly visible and flat. The overall mood is warm, intimate and reflective, evoking the feeling ofa first-birthday memory and the visual contrast between past and present. The final output must be photorealistic, clean and natural, with no artificial effects or stylization.
