پرامپت فارسی

Use the provided character sheet as the strict identity reference. Preserve exact facial identity, skin tone, facial proportions, hair color and texture, body proportions, age and distinctive features. The result must clearly be the same person from the character sheet. Create an expensive lifestyle moment that feels accidentally captured like a spontaneous Instagram Story, not a professional photoshoot. Photorealistic flagship-iPhone close-up portrait with imperfect framing, no perfect symmetry and no staged feeling. The character stands in a green summer meadow with tall grass, wheat-like plants and small wildflowers. Style the hair into two loose braids while preserving the original hair characteristics. Weave small fresh daisies into both braids, with a few loose strands around the face. Makeup: black winged eyeliner extending slightly toward the temples, defined lashes, glossy soft-pink lips, gentle blush and subtle sun-kissed skin. Wardrobe: feminine white cotton top with delicate ruffles and thin straps. The character looks directly into the camera with a playful, confident expression and subtle half-smile. A summer breeze moves strands of hair across part of the face. Strong natural sunlight, high contrast, long shadows, slightly dark exposure. Authentic smartphone sensor noise, natural sharpening and realistic phone-camera dynamic range. Photorealistic, realistic pores and hair strands, no studio lighting, no perfect symmetry, no plastic skin, no excessive retouching, no text, no watermark.
