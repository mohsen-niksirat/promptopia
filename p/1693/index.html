An ultra-photorealistic 9:16 vertical luxury café lifestyle portrait. Preserve the reference woman's face, features, skin tone, expression, and dark brown wavy hair 100% unchanged beneath a charcoal-black baseball cap with a small gold logo. Scene & Pose: Sitting casually on a concrete bench in a minimalist coffee shop with gray concrete walls and wooden furniture. Body angled slightly, left hand holding an iced matcha latte (black lid and straw) on a round wooden table, right hand raised naturally with anatomically correct fingers. A smartphone sits on the table beside the drink. Outfit & Accessories: Wearing modern lime-yellow sunglasses over her eyes, a sheer summer chiffon lime-yellow shirt over a covered high-neck bodysuit matching her light-wash relaxed straight-leg cuffed denim jeans, paired with simple stylish white sneakers. Accessories include a smartwatch with a light band, silver rings, silver bracelet, delicate necklace, and a compact gray patterned crossbody bag. Lighting & Camera: Soft natural window daylight, gentle realistic shadows, and reflections. Shot on iPhone 16 Pro Max, 48MP, portrait mode, shallow depth of field, 8K quality. Extremely realistic skin with visible pores and natural textures, no beauty filters, no smoothing, no plastic appearance. Negative Prompt: cartoon, CGI, 3D render, plastic skin, beauty filter, altered face, extra fingers, missing fingers, deformed hands, bad anatomy, low quality, text, watermark.
