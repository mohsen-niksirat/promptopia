پرامپت فارسی

Create an ultra-photorealistic cinematic black-and-white vertical 9:16 full-body studio portrait using the two uploaded reference images as permanent identity anchors. IDENTITY LOCK — HIGHEST PRIORITY Reference 1 = father. Reference 2 = son. Preserve their exact real identities with maximum facial accuracy: facial structure, head shape, age, skin texture, hairline, hairstyle, eyebrows, eyes, nose, lips, jaw, ears, beard/mustache, natural asymmetry, body proportions and all distinctive features. Both must remain instantly recognizable. No beautification, rejuvenation, aging, reshaping or generic faces. COMPOSITION A powerful, symmetrical father-and-son mafia-style studio portrait conveying authority, dignity, family legacy and wealth. Father: seated perfectly centered in the foreground on a large luxurious dark leather armchair with tall back, broad armrests and realistic hand-carved dark wood details. Upright commanding posture, straight spine, squared shoulders, open chest, chin level with a subtle confident lift. Legs naturally apart, polished black leather shoes firmly on the floor. Calm, serious, controlled expression, looking directly at camera. He wears a perfectly tailored black suit, black dress shirt open 2–3 buttons, no tie, with an elegant silver/white-gold chain necklace. 2–3 tasteful luxury signet rings. A premium hand-carved black ebony cane stands perfectly vertical between his legs, tip firmly on the floor. Heavy realistic carved handle, ornamental detailing and subtle silver inlay. Both hands rest naturally and firmly over the upper handle in a symmetrical, authoritative pose. In his right hand, he also holds a magnificent prayer-bead strand made of very large, individually visible polished black onyx/dark agate beads. The beads hang naturally beside the cane with realistic gravity, weight, reflections and texture. Keep beads, fingers and cane clearly separated with anatomically correct hands. Son: standing directly behind the father, perfectly centered with the chair. Tall, strong and protective posture; squared shoulders, open chest, arms firmly crossed. Serious, composed, self-assured expression, looking directly at camera. All-black tailored suit, black trousers, fitted open-collar black shirt, no tie. Polished black leather shoes subtly visible on both sides of the chair. LIGHTING / STUDIO Dark professional photography studio, seamless charcoal-black background and subtly visible dark floor. Premium cinematic chiaroscuro: - Large soft key light from upper front-side - Controlled fill preserving facial detail - Subtle rim light separating black clothing from background - Deep but detailed shadows, rich monochrome tonal range - Natural highlights on leather, carved wood, cane, rings, necklace and beads CAMERA / QUALITY Professional full-frame camera, 50mm–70mm portrait lens, centered frontal framing, chest-height camera with a subtle low-angle perspective. Deep focus keeping both faces, hands, cane and beads sharp. Extremely realistic skin pores, beard, fabric, leather, carved wood and stone textures. Ultra-photorealistic cinematic monochrome photography, HDR, refined contrast, subtle authentic film grain, editorial magazine quality, realistic anatomy, 8K detail. NEGATIVE No identity drift, facial alteration, beautification, age change, generic faces, weak/slouched posture, off-center son, crooked cane, small/plastic beads, deformed hands, extra fingers, merged fingers, duplicated limbs, asymmetry, casual clothing, ties, hats, sunglasses, weapons, cigar, smile, fantasy throne, excessive ornaments, color, text, logos, watermark, cartoon or painterly look.
