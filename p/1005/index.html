پرامپت فارسی

Use refrence photo and create A vertical photorealistic night portrait in the aesthetics of a smartphone photo with a flash: a young adult woman photographed on a dark beach by the water from the back in a three-quarter turn, the shot from the head to the upper thighs, the figure is located slightly below the center, she turns her head over her left shoulder and looks directly into the camera with a calm, confident gaze. Her hair, slicked back and tied in a small high bun, secured with a light elastic band with a small yellow accent; her face with neat eyebrows, soft glamorous makeup, and glossy reddish lips. She wears long dangling earrings, a dark brown crop tops, cream high-waisted denim jeans; long light nails are visible at the hip. The camera is close, a slight angle at about hip level, direct frontal flash gives bright pink highlights on the skin, harsh shadows, slight overexposure of the model, and strong separation from the almost black background. Below, textured wet sand, behind it dark calm water and a faint line of breaking waves, to the right distant city lights or a pier with vertical reflections, to the left soft misty glow, deep black sky, high contrast, warm night atmosphere, a palette of black, cold blue water, pale denim, dark brown, and warm city lights.
