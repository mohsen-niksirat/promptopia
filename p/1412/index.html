پرامپت فارسی

Create a premium double-exposure portrait of the uploaded man. The composition should match the reference image exactly: a large blended head-and-shoulders portrait occupying the upper 70% of the frame, softly fading into a dreamy misty background. The man is looking slightly to the side with a confident, calm expression. He has stylish black sunglasses, well-groomed hair, and wears a fitted black shirt. In the lower center, place a smaller, full-body version of the same man standing confidently, wearing a fashionable black outfit with modern styling. Keep the body size similar to the reference image. The large portrait and the smaller standing figure should blend together seamlessly through soft clouds, mist, and cinematic light effects. Use luxury fashion magazine photography style, ultra-realistic details, soft atmospheric glow, subtle depth, elegant color grading, premium Photoshop composite artwork, high-end editorial look, dramatic yet clean lighting, smooth skin details, realistic fabric texture, and a sophisticated masculine aesthetic. Keep a thin, elegant frame around the image. The background should be dreamy, airy, and lightly textured with soft clouds and haze. No text, no logos, no extra objects. Vertical 9:16 ratio suitable for Instagram and Facebook posts.
